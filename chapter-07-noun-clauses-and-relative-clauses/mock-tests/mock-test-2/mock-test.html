Mock Test - 2

Full marks : 30Time : 60 minutes

  1. Answer all the questions. The marks for each question are shown on the right.
  2. Section A: 6 questions, 1 mark each · Section B: 4 questions, 3 marks each · Section C: 3 questions, 4 marks each.
  3. In Section A choose the correct option; in Sections B and C write complete sentences.

ASection A : Multiple Choice Questions6 × 1 = 6

  1. In "It is certain that he will pass", the clause "that he will pass" is:

    1. (a) the subject
    2. (b) the object
    3. (c) in apposition to "it"
    4. (d) a relative clause
    [1] Answer →

  2. In which sentence can "that" NOT fill the blank?

    1. (a) The book ___ I read was good.
    2. (b) Delhi, ___ is our capital, is crowded.
    3. (c) The man ___ came here is my uncle.
    4. (d) All ___ glitters is not gold.
    [1] Answer →

  3. In "Satish scored a goal in the last minute, which was fortunate", "which" refers to:

    1. (a) Satish
    2. (b) a goal
    3. (c) the whole main clause
    4. (d) the last minute
    [1] Answer →

  4. Choose the correct sentence.

    1. (a) The man whom I met him was kind.
    2. (b) The man whom I met was kind.
    3. (c) The man who I met him was kind.
    4. (d) The man which I met was kind.
    [1] Answer →

  5. In "He posted the letter after he had sealed it", the clause "after he had sealed it" is a:

    1. (a) finite subordinate clause
    2. (b) non-finite clause
    3. (c) main clause
    4. (d) coordinate clause
    [1] Answer →

  6. In "Whether he will come is uncertain", the noun clause works as the:

    1. (a) subject
    2. (b) object
    3. (c) complement
    4. (d) object of a preposition
    [1] Answer →

BSection B : Short Answer Questions4 × 3 = 12

  1. Explain the difference in meaning:
    (a) The students who had finished their work went home.
    (b) The students, who had finished their work, went home.

    [3] Answer →

  2. Complete each sentence with a noun clause doing the job given in brackets:
    (a) _____ is a mystery. (subject)
    (b) I wonder _____ . (object)
    (c) The question is _____ . (complement)

    [3] Answer →

  3. Combine each pair, using a preposition + which / whom:
    (a) This is the chair. He was sitting on it.
    (b) The girl is my friend. I was talking to her.
    (c) The company is very large. My father works for it.

    [3] Answer →

  4. Drop the relative pronoun where possible; otherwise write "not possible" and say why:
    (a) The film that we saw was boring.
    (b) The man who lives next door is a pilot.
    (c) Rita, whom you met yesterday, is my cousin.

    [3] Answer →
