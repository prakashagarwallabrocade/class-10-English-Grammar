7. Noun Clauses and Relative Clauses Easy–Medium

Mock Test - 1

Full marks : 30Time : 45 minutes

  1. Answer all the questions. The marks for each question are shown on the right.
  2. Section A: 10 questions, 1 mark each · Section B: 5 questions, 2 marks each · Section C: 2 questions, 5 marks each.
  3. In Section A choose the correct option; in Sections B and C write complete sentences.

ASection A : Multiple Choice Questions10 × 1 = 10

  1. "in the morning" is a:

    1. (a) phrase
    2. (b) clause
    3. (c) sentence
    4. (d) main clause
    [1] Answer →

  2. The book _____ you gave me is interesting.

    1. (a) which
    2. (b) who
    3. (c) whose
    4. (d) where
    [1] Answer →

  3. She is the girl _____ won the prize.

    1. (a) who
    2. (b) which
    3. (c) whom
    4. (d) whose
    [1] Answer →

  4. I know _____ he lives.

    1. (a) where
    2. (b) which
    3. (c) whose
    4. (d) whom
    [1] Answer →

  5. This is the boy _____ father is a doctor.

    1. (a) whose
    2. (b) who
    3. (c) whom
    4. (d) which
    [1] Answer →

  6. In "What she said is true", the noun clause works as the:

    1. (a) subject
    2. (b) object
    3. (c) complement
    4. (d) object of a preposition
    [1] Answer →

  7. In "I hope that he will come", the clause "that he will come" works as the:

    1. (a) subject
    2. (b) object
    3. (c) complement
    4. (d) object of a preposition
    [1] Answer →

  8. The house _____ I was born is near the river.

    1. (a) where
    2. (b) which
    3. (c) who
    4. (d) whose
    [1] Answer →

  9. Which sentence has a non-defining relative clause?

    1. (a) The man who came yesterday is my uncle.
    2. (b) My father, who is a doctor, lives in Delhi.
    3. (c) The book that I bought is new.
    4. (d) I know a girl who sings well.
    [1] Answer →

  10. In which sentence can the relative pronoun be dropped?

    1. (a) The boy who won is my cousin.
    2. (b) The pen that I lost was red.
    3. (c) Rita, who sings well, is here.
    4. (d) The dog which barked ran away.
    [1] Answer →

    (a) I met a man. He was blind.
    (b) This is the bag. I bought it yesterday.

  2. Say whether each is a phrase or a clause:
    (a) because it was raining
    (b) because of the rain

    [2] Answer →

  3. Pick out the noun clause and say how it works:
    (a) I know that he is honest.
    (b) The fact is that he is lazy.

    [2] Answer →

  4. Fill in the blanks with relative pronouns:
    (a) The man _____ car was stolen went to the police.
    (b) The pen _____ you gave me writes well.

    [2] Answer →

  5. Put commas where they are necessary:
    (a) My mother who is a nurse works at night.
