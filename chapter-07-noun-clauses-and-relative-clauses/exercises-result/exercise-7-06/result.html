1Question

The children who are playing cricket live in this locality.

Rules used

  • Dropping the relative pronoun

    The relative pronoun can be dropped only when it is the object of the verb (or preposition) in a defining relative clause. It cannot be dropped when it is the subject, or in a non-defining clause.

    📖 Relative Clauses
  1. 'who' is the subject of 'are playing'.

Answer: Cannot be dropped: The children who are playing cricket live in this locality.
