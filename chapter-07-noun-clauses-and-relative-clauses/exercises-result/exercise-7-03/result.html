1Question

It is not clear who has done it.

Rules used

  • Noun clauses

    A noun clause does the work of a noun: subject (What he says is correct), object (Radha said that she was happy), complement (The problem is that we have no money), object of a preposition (Pay attention to what I say), or in apposition (The news that he has come is true / It is true that he has come). It begins with a wh-word, if or that.

    📖 Noun Clauses
  1. 'It' stands for the clause: Who has done it is not clear.

Answer: It is not clear who has done it. — in apposition to 'It'
