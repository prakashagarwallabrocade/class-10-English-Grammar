iQuestion

A green grocer is a person who sells fruits and vegetables.

Rules used

  • Defining and non-defining

    A defining clause tells which one we mean and is essential (no commas). A non-defining clause adds extra information about a person or thing already known and is set off by commas; it cannot use 'that' and can refer to a whole clause (…, which was fortunate).

    📖 Relative Clauses (REMEMBER)
  1. It tells us which kind of person.

Answer: A green grocer is a person who sells fruits and vegetables. — D
