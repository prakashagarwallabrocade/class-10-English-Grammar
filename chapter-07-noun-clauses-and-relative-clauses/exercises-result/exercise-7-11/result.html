1Question

The house in ——— I lived as a child has been pulled down now.

Rules used

  • Relative clauses

    A relative (adjective) clause describes the noun before it and begins with who, whom, which, that, whose (or where for a place). To combine two sentences, put the relative pronoun after the common noun.

    📖 Relative Clauses
  1. After a preposition, a thing: which (not that).

Answer: The house in which I lived as a child has been pulled down now.

2Question

Rules used (as above)
  • Relative clauses

    A relative (adjective) clause describes the noun before it and begins with who, whom, which, that, whose (or where for a place). To combine two sentences, put the relative pronoun after the common noun.

    📖 Relative Clauses
  1. A person, non-defining (commas): who.

Answer: My grandmother, who was an extraordinary woman, lived to the age of one hundred.
