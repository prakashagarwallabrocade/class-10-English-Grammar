1Question

I cannot express how sorry I am.

Rules used

  • Main, coordinate and subordinate clauses

    The main clause can stand alone. A coordinate clause is joined to it by and / but / or. A subordinate clause (beginning with that, what, how, when, who, if, before …) cannot stand alone.

    📖 Noun Clauses and Relative Clauses (introduction)
  1. The how-clause is the object of 'express'.
  2. Draw a double line under the main clause and a single line under the subordinate clause.

Answer: Main: I cannot express · Subordinate: how sorry I am
