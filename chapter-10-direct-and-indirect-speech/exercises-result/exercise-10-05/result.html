(i)Question

What is the name of your sister’s husband ?

Rules used

  • Questions in indirect speech

    ask / enquire / want to know + (person) + wh-word (or if / whether for Yes-No questions) + subject + verb; no 'that', no question mark, statement word order, tense changes as usual.

    📖 (I) Questions and (J) Yes-No questions in Indirect Speech
  • Tense changes after a past reporting verb

    present simple → past simple · present progressive → past progressive · present perfect (progressive) → past perfect (progressive) · past simple → past perfect · past progressive → past perfect progressive · will → would, shall → should / would, can → could, may → might, must → must / had to / would have to.

    📖 (G) and (b) Change of modal auxiliaries
  1. Wh-question → wh-word + subject + verb; is → was; your → my.

Answer: Mahesh asked me what the name of my sister’s husband was.
