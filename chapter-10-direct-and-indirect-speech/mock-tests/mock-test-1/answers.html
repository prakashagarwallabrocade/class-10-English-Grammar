1QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

He said, “I am tired.” → He said that he _____ tired.

  1. (a) was ✓
  2. (b) is
  3. (c) has been
  4. (d) will be

Answer: (a) He said, “I am tired.” → He said that he was tired.

Show the explanation
  1. After a past reporting verb, the present simple "am" becomes the past simple "was"; I → he.

3QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

“Where do you live?” he asked me. → He asked me where I _____.

  1. (a) lived ✓
  2. (b) live
  3. (c) do live
  4. (d) did live

Answer: (a) “Where do you live?” he asked me. → He asked me where I lived.

Show the explanation
  1. The question becomes a statement (no do / did); present simple → past simple; you → I.

  1. (a) to close ✓
  2. (b) close
  3. (c) closing
  4. (d) closed

Answer: (a) He said to me, “Close the door.” → He told me to close the door.

Show the explanation
  1. An order: tell + person + to + V1.

5QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

The teacher said, “The earth is round.” → The teacher said that the earth _____ round.

  1. (a) is ✓
  2. (b) was
  3. (c) had been
  4. (d) will be

Answer: (a) The teacher said, “The earth is round.” → The teacher said that the earth is round.

Show the explanation
  1. A scientific truth does not change its tense.

6QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

She asked me, “Are you happy?” → She asked me _____ I was happy.

  1. (a) if ✓
  2. (b) that
  3. (c) what
  4. (d) which

Answer: (a) She asked me, “Are you happy?” → She asked me if I was happy.

Show the explanation
  1. A Yes-No question is reported with if / whether, never "that".

7QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

He said, “I came yesterday.” → He said that he had come _____.

  1. (a) the day before ✓
  2. (b) yesterday
  3. (c) tomorrow
  4. (d) today

Answer: (a) He said, “I came yesterday.” → He said that he had come the day before.

Show the explanation
  1. yesterday → the day before (the previous day); came → had come.

  3. (c) no
  4. (d) not

Answer: (a) He said to me, “Don’t shout.” → He told me not to shout.

9QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

  3. (c) has been
  4. (d) had been

10QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

“What a beautiful flower!” she said. → She exclaimed that it was _____ beautiful flower.

  1. (a) a very ✓
  2. (b) so a
  3. (c) how a
  4. (d) what a

Answer: (a) “What a beautiful flower!” she said. → She exclaimed that it was a very beautiful flower.

11QuestionSection B · 2 marksEasy

Change into indirect speech:
(a) He said, “I like mangoes.”
(b) She said, “I am reading.”

Answer: (a) He said that he liked mangoes. (b) She said that she was reading.

Show the explanation
  1. like → liked; am reading → was reading; I → he / she.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (G)

12QuestionSection B · 2 marksEasy

Change into indirect speech:
(a) He said to me, “Are you ready?”
(b) She said to him, “Where are you going?”

Answer: (a) He asked me if I was ready. (b) She asked him where he was going.

Show the explanation
  1. said to → asked; Yes-No question → if; wh-question → wh-word + subject + verb.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (I)

13QuestionSection B · 2 marksMedium

Change into indirect speech:
(a) The teacher said to the boys, “Sit down.”
(b) Mother said to me, “Don’t play in the sun.”

Answer: (a) The teacher told the boys to sit down. (b) Mother advised me not to play in the sun.

Show the explanation
  1. Commands and advice: told / advised + person + (not) to + V1.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (K)

14QuestionSection B · 2 marksEasy

What do these words become in indirect speech?
(a) today
(b) tomorrow

Answer: (a) that day (b) the next day (the following day)

Show the explanation
  1. Time words move away from the moment of speaking when the report is made later.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (a)

15QuestionSection B · 2 marksMedium

Correct the errors:
(a) He asked me where was I going.
(b) She said me that she was ill.

Answer: (a) He asked me where I was going. (b) She told me that she was ill.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) Reported questions use statement word order. (b) "say" takes no person as object; use "told me" (or "said to me").
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (I)

16QuestionSection C · 5 marksEasy

Change into indirect speech:
(a) Ravi said, “I am hungry.”
(b) Sita said, “I have finished my work.”
(c) He said to me, “I will help you.”
(d) They said, “We are going to Puri.”
(e) She said, “I can swim.”

Answer: (a) Ravi said that he was hungry. (b) Sita said that she had finished her work. (c) He told me that he would help me. (d) They said that they were going to Puri. (e) She said that she could swim.

Show the explanation
  1. am → was; have finished → had finished; will → would; are going → were going; can → could.
  2. Change the persons: I → he / she, my → her, you → me, we → they.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (G)

17QuestionSection C · 5 marksMedium

Report this conversation in indirect speech:
Teacher : “Have you done your homework?”
Student : “Yes, sir.”
Teacher : “Show it to me.”

Answer: The teacher asked the student if he had done his homework. The student respectfully replied that he had. The teacher told him to show it to him.

Show the explanation
  1. A Yes-No question → asked if …; have done → had done.
  2. "Yes, sir" → replied (respectfully) that he had — subject + auxiliary.
  3. A command → told him to + V1.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (J)
