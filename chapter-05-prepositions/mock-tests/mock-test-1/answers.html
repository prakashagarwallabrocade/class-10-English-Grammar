6QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

She has been waiting _____ two hours.

  1. (a) for ✓
  2. (b) since
  3. (c) from
  4. (d) by

Answer: (a) She has been waiting for two hours.

Show the explanation
  1. "two hours" is a period of time: for. ("since" needs a point of time, like since 6 o’clock.)

10QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

This ring is made _____ gold.

  1. (a) of ✓
  2. (b) from
  3. (c) by
  4. (d) with

Answer: (a) This ring is made of gold.

Show the explanation
  1. made of = the material can still be seen (gold); made from = the material is changed (butter from milk).

11QuestionSection B · 2 marksEasy

Fill in the blanks with suitable prepositions:
(a) The dictionary is _____ the shelf.
(b) He is waiting _____ the bus-stop.

Answer: (a) The dictionary is on the shelf. (b) He is waiting at the bus-stop.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) Touching a surface: on. (b) An exact point: at.

12QuestionSection B · 2 marksEasy

Fill in the blanks:
(a) It has been raining _____ morning.
(b) I will finish the work _____ Friday at the latest.

Answer: (a) It has been raining since morning. (b) I will finish the work by Friday at the latest.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) A point of time with the perfect: since. (b) The latest time: by.

15QuestionSection B · 2 marksMedium

Choose the correct preposition:
(a) The old man died (of / from) cholera.
(b) Butter is made (of / from) milk.

Answer: (a) died of cholera (b) made from milk

Show the explanation
  1. (a) die of an illness; die from a wound or other cause.
  2. (b) made from: the milk is changed into butter.

16QuestionSection C · 5 marksEasy

Fill in the blanks with suitable prepositions:
Rina lives _____ Bhubaneswar. Every morning she goes to school _____ bus. Her school starts _____ 8 o’clock. _____ Sundays she does not go to school. She plays _____ the park with her friends.

Answer: Rina lives in Bhubaneswar. Every morning she goes to school by bus. Her school starts at 8 o’clock. On Sundays she does not go to school. She plays in the park with her friends.

Show the explanation
  1. in + a city; by + vehicle without an article; at + clock time; on + days; in + an enclosed area (the park).
← Back to the question 📖 Section: 3

17QuestionSection C · 5 marksMedium

Fill in the blanks with the right prepositions:
(a) Let us congratulate him _____ his success.
(b) She is fond _____ music.
(c) He is interested _____ painting.
(d) I agree _____ you on this point.
(e) Please don’t interfere _____ my work.

Answer: (a) congratulate him on (b) fond of (c) interested in (d) agree with (e) interfere in

Show the explanation
  1. These verbs and adjectives take fixed prepositions: congratulate on, fond of, interested in, agree with (a person), interfere in.
