1Place: at, in, on10 examples

at for an exact point, in for inside an area or space, on for a surface or a line.

  1. Wait for me at the bus stop.

    at an exact point

  2. There was a big crowd at the station.

    at a place seen as a point

  3. My uncle lives in Sambalpur.

    in a town or city

  4. The keys are in my bag.

    in inside a container

  5. The children are playing in the garden.

    in within an area

  6. There is a calendar on the wall.

    on a surface

  7. Cuttack is on the Mahanadi.

    on beside a river (a line)

  8. Our flat is on the third floor.

    on a floor of a building

  9. She is at school now, but her books are in the school library.

    at / in at = attending; in = inside the building

  10. I met him at the wedding in Puri.

    at / in at an event; in a town

2Other prepositions of place10 examples

over / above, under / below / beneath, through, across, along, behind, before, between / among, against, opposite and in front of.

  1. A kite was flying above the trees.

    above higher than, not touching

  2. She held an umbrella over her head.

    over covering, directly above

  3. The cat is hiding under the bed.

    under directly beneath

  4. The temperature fell below zero.

    below lower than a level

  5. The train passed through a long tunnel.

    through from one end to the other, inside

  6. We swam across the river.

    across from one side to the other

  7. Trees grow along both sides of the road.

    along following a line

  8. Divide the sweets between the two brothers, and the fruit among all the children.

    between / among between two; among more than two

  9. He leaned his bicycle against the wall.

    against touching and pressing on

  10. The bank is opposite the post office, and a car is parked in front of it.

    opposite / in front of opposite = facing across; in front of = just ahead of

3Prepositions of direction10 examples

to, towards, for, into, onto, from, off and out of show movement.

  1. We are going to Konark tomorrow.

    to the destination

  2. The dog ran towards me.

    towards in the direction of

  3. The train for Delhi leaves at noon.

    for the intended destination

  4. She jumped into the pool.

    into movement to the inside

  5. The cat jumped onto the table.

    onto movement to a surface

  6. He came home from the office at six.

    from the starting point

  7. The boy fell off the wall.

    off away from a surface

  8. She took a pen out of her bag.

    out of from the inside

  9. Get out of the car and walk into the shop.

    out of / into opposite movements

  10. He set out for Kolkata but went towards the sea first.

    for / towards destination vs. direction

4Prepositions of time10 examples

at for clock times and festivals, in for months, years and parts of the day, on for days and dates; by, for, since, from … to, between, during, in time and on time.

  1. The school opens at 10 o’clock.

    at a clock time

  2. We visit our grandparents at Dussehra.

    at a festival period

  3. I was born in 2010, in the month of June.

    in a year, a month

  4. He goes for a walk in the evening.

    in a part of the day

  5. The results will be out on Monday, on 15th May.

    on a day, a date

  6. Please submit the form by Friday.

    by not later than

  7. She has lived here for ten years / since 2014.

    for / since for a period; since a starting point

  8. The shop is open from 9 am to 8 pm.

    from … to start and end

  9. Nobody spoke during the lecture.

    during all through a period

  10. The train arrived on time, and we reached the hall in time for the show.

    on time / in time on time = punctual; in time = early enough

5Travel, communication, payment, instrument and agency10 examples

by + means of travel or communication (no article); on / in when there is an article; with an instrument; by an agent.

  1. My father goes to the office by bus.

    by + transport no article after by

  2. We travelled to Delhi by train and came back by air.

    by + transport by train, by air

  3. She came on her bicycle; he came on foot.

    on + article / foot on with an article, and on foot

  4. They went to the airport in a taxi.

    in + car / taxi in for a car or boat with an article

  5. I sent the photos by e-mail.

    by + communication by post, by e-mail, by phone

  6. We heard the news on the radio and watched it on television.

    on + radio / TV on the radio, on television

  7. Can I pay by card, or must I pay in cash?

    by / in + payment by cheque, by card; in cash

  8. He cut the rope with a knife.

    with + instrument the tool used

  9. Please fill in the form in ink.

    in + material in ink, in pencil

  10. The play was written by Shakespeare.

    by + agent the doer in a passive sentence

6Idiomatic uses of prepositions10 examples

Fixed phrases with at, by, in, on, out of, under and within.

  1. I learnt the poem by heart.

    by heart from memory

  2. I took your umbrella by mistake.

    by mistake not on purpose

  3. He broke the glass on purpose.

    on purpose deliberately

  4. Come here at once!

    at once immediately

  5. The building was on fire.

    on fire burning

  6. My uncle is out of work these days.

    out of work without a job

  7. The lift is out of order.

    out of order not working

  8. I am in a hurry; let’s talk later.

    in a hurry rushing

  9. The fire was soon brought under control.

    under control managed, not spreading

  10. At last the rain stopped.

    at last finally, after a long wait

7Verbs followed by definite prepositions10 examples

Some verbs take a fixed preposition; changing it changes the meaning.

  1. She inquired after my mother’s health.

    inquire after ask about someone’s well-being

  2. The guard warned us against entering the forest at night.

    warn against advise not to do something

  3. Many people died of hunger during the famine.

    die of an illness or hunger

  4. The baby depends on its mother.

    depend on rely on

  5. Do you believe in ghosts?

    believe in think something exists

  6. I agree with you, but they did not agree to our plan.

    agree with / agree to with a person; to a proposal

  7. This song reminds me of my childhood.

    remind of make one remember

  8. The table is made of wood; paper is made from wood.

    made of / made from of = material still seen; from = material changed

  9. The police accused him of stealing.

    accuse of charge with a wrong

  10. She apologised to the teacher for coming late.

    apologise to / for to a person; for the fault

8Adjectives followed by definite prepositions10 examples

Some adjectives take a fixed preposition.

  1. My little brother is afraid of the dark.

    afraid of frightened by

  2. Rina is good at mathematics.

    good at skilful in

  3. Are you interested in music?

    interested in wanting to know more

  4. The teacher was pleased with our work.

    pleased with satisfied

  5. Smoking is harmful to health.

    harmful to causing harm

  6. He is proud of his daughter’s success.

    proud of feeling pleased about

  7. Our school is famous for its football team.

    famous for known because of

  8. This book is different from that one.

    different from not the same

  9. We are grateful to you for your help.

    grateful to / for to a person; for a thing

  10. She is fond of sweets and keen on dancing.

    fond of / keen on liking something

9Nouns followed by definite prepositions10 examples

Some nouns take a fixed preposition.

  1. She has a great interest in painting.

    interest in noun + in

  2. There is no need for worry.

    need for noun + for

  3. I have no doubt about his honesty.

    doubt about noun + about

  4. Rabi has a liking for sweets.

    liking for noun + for

  5. The cause of the accident is not known.

    cause of noun + of

  6. There has been an increase in prices.

    increase in noun + in (the thing that grows)

  7. What is the reason for your absence?

    reason for noun + for

  8. Who has the key to this cupboard?

    key to noun + to

  9. The child showed great respect for his elders.

    respect for noun + for

  10. Her answer to my question was clear.

    answer to noun + to
