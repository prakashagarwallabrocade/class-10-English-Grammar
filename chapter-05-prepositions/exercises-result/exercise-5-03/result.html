(i)Question

He went to America _____ an aeroplane.

Rules used

  • Means of travel

    by + vehicle with no article (by bus, by air); on / in + a, the (on a plane, on the train, in a car).

    📖 4.1
  1. With an article (an aeroplane) we use on, not by ('by air' / 'by aeroplane' has no article).

Answer: He went to America on an aeroplane.

(ii)Question

He went to Howarh _____ the Coromondal Express.

Rules used (as above)
  • Means of travel

    by + vehicle with no article (by bus, by air); on / in + a, the (on a plane, on the train, in a car).

    📖 4.1
  1. A particular train with 'the': on.

Answer: He went to Howarh on the Coromondal Express.

(iii)Question

He sent the books _____ registered parcel.

Rules used

  • Communication and payment

    by post, by telephone, by registered parcel; by cheque but in cash.

    📖 4.2
  1. A means of sending: by.

Answer: He sent the books by registered parcel.

(iv)Question

He dug the pit _____ a shovel.

Rules used

  • Instrument

    with + the tool used (with a pen, with a shovel); but in ink, in pencil (the material).

    📖 4.3
  1. The tool used: with.

Answer: He dug the pit with a shovel.

(v)Question

He talked to his friend _____ telephone.

Rules used (as above)
  • Communication and payment

    by post, by telephone, by registered parcel; by cheque but in cash.

    📖 4.2
  1. A means of communication without an article: by (the book’s own example).

Answer: He talked to his friend by telephone.

(vi)Question

He paid his dues _____ cash.

Rules used (as above)
  • Communication and payment

    by post, by telephone, by registered parcel; by cheque but in cash.

    📖 4.2
  1. Payment: in cash (but by cheque).

Answer: He paid his dues in cash.

(vii)Question

If you haven’t got any cash on you, you can pay _____ cheque.

Rules used (as above)
  • Communication and payment

    by post, by telephone, by registered parcel; by cheque but in cash.

    📖 4.2
  1. Payment: by cheque.

Answer: If you haven’t got any cash on you, you can pay by cheque.

(viii)Question

The answer was written _____ a pencil.

Rules used (as above)
  • Instrument

    with + the tool used (with a pen, with a shovel); but in ink, in pencil (the material).

    📖 4.3
  1. 'a pencil' is the tool: with.

Answer: The answer was written with a pencil.

(ix)Question

The answer was written _____ pencil.

Rules used (as above)
  • Instrument

    with + the tool used (with a pen, with a shovel); but in ink, in pencil (the material).

    📖 4.3
  1. 'pencil' without an article is the material: in (like 'in ink').

Answer: The answer was written in pencil.
