(i)Question

The young men are swimming _____ the sea.

Rules used

  • in (place)

    Inside a larger area or a building: in a city, in a factory, in the corner of a room, in the sea.

    📖 2.2. In
  1. Inside a large body of water.

Answer: The young men are swimming in the sea.

(ii)Question

There is a label _____ the medicine bottle.

Rules used

  • on (place)

    Touching a surface; a place of work that is not a building (on a farm); close to a river or coast (London is on the Thames); on a hillside.

    📖 2.3. On
  1. Touching the surface of the bottle.

Answer: There is a label on the medicine bottle.

(iii)Question

There is a TV set _____ the corner of the room.

Rules used (as above)
  • in (place)

    Inside a larger area or a building: in a city, in a factory, in the corner of a room, in the sea.

    📖 2.2. In
  1. Inside the room ('in the corner of the room', as in the book).

Answer: There is a TV set in the corner of the room.

(iv)Question

London is _____ the Thames.

Rules used (as above)
  • on (place)

    Touching a surface; a place of work that is not a building (on a farm); close to a river or coast (London is on the Thames); on a hillside.

    📖 2.3. On
  1. A city beside a river: on (like 'Haridwar is on the Ganges').

Answer: London is on the Thames.

(v)Question

John was brought up _____ a farm, but at the age of sixty he was sent to work _____ a shop.

Rules used (as above)
  • on (place)

    Touching a surface; a place of work that is not a building (on a farm); close to a river or coast (London is on the Thames); on a hillside.

    📖 2.3. On
  • in (place)

    Inside a larger area or a building: in a city, in a factory, in the corner of a room, in the sea.

    📖 2.2. In
  1. A farm is not a building (on); a shop is a building where one works (in).

Answer: John was brought up on a farm, but at the age of sixty he was sent to work in a shop.

(vi)Question

When we visited Hyderabad, we stayed _____ the Justa Hotel.

Rules used

  • at (place)

    An exact point: an address, a station, a door, a corner, a building as a meeting point.

    📖 2.1. AT
  1. A particular hotel as a point where we stayed.

Answer: When we visited Hyderabad, we stayed at the Justa Hotel.

(vii)Question

I would prefer to work _____ a factory.

Rules used (as above)
  • in (place)

    Inside a larger area or a building: in a city, in a factory, in the corner of a room, in the sea.

    📖 2.2. In
  1. A place of work that is a building.

Answer: I would prefer to work in a factory.

(viii)Question

He told me that he lived _____ 25 Ashok Nagar, Bhubaneswar.

Rules used (as above)
  • at (place)

    An exact point: an address, a station, a door, a corner, a building as a meeting point.

    📖 2.1. AT
  1. A full address with a house number: at.

Answer: He told me that he lived at 25 Ashok Nagar, Bhubaneswar.

(ix)Question

My father is employed as a cashier _____ the local branch of the Allahabad Bank.

Rules used (as above)
  • at (place)

    An exact point: an address, a station, a door, a corner, a building as a meeting point.

    📖 2.1. AT
  1. A particular workplace named as a point ('at the public library').

Answer: My father is employed as a cashier at the local branch of the Allahabad Bank.

(x)Question

He was _____ me at school. (Suggesting ‘lower than’)

Rules used

  • under / below / beneath

    under = directly lower, or less than / under someone’s authority; below = lower than (also in rank).

    📖 2.5. Under, Below, and Beneath
  1. 'lower than' in class or rank: below.

Answer: He was below me at school.

(xi)Question

My hat fell _____ a chair as I was leaving the room.

Rules used

  • Direction

    off = down from a surface; for = towards a destination (set off for home, left for home).

    📖 2.15–2.16
  1. Down from a surface: off.

Answer: My hat fell off a chair as I was leaving the room.

(xii)Question

Rules used (as above)
  • under / below / beneath

    under = directly lower, or less than / under someone’s authority; below = lower than (also in rank).

    📖 2.5. Under, Below, and Beneath
  1. Under his authority.

Answer: He has ten men working under him.

(xiii)Question

They were standing _____ the hillside.

Rules used (as above)
  • on (place)

    Touching a surface; a place of work that is not a building (on a farm); close to a river or coast (London is on the Thames); on a hillside.

    📖 2.3. On
  1. On the surface of the slope.

(xiv)Question

They were walking together _____ the beach.

Rules used

  • along

    In a line from one end to the other, or in the direction of the length of something.

    📖 2.8. Along
  1. In the direction of the length of the beach.

Answer: They were walking together along the beach.

(xv)Question

He waited half an hour _____ the corner of the street.

Rules used (as above)
  • at (place)

    An exact point: an address, a station, a door, a corner, a building as a meeting point.

    📖 2.1. AT
  1. An exact point: 'at the corner of the street'.

Answer: He waited half an hour at the corner of the street.

(xvi)Question

She divided the cake _____ her five children.

Rules used

  • between / among

    between = two persons or things; among = three or more.

    📖 2.11. Between / Among
  1. More than two people: among.

Answer: She divided the cake among her five children.

(xvii)Question

I can’t see any difference _____ these two books.

Rules used

  • between / among

    between = two persons or things; among = three or more.

    📖 2.11. Between / Among
  • Noun + fixed preposition

    relationship with, a fall in, difference between, reaction to, progress in, discussion on, familiarity with, admiration for, agreement with, impression on.

    📖 8. Nouns followed by definite prepositions
  1. Two things: between.

Answer: I can’t see any difference between these two books.

    📖 2.1. AT
  1. An exact point: 'at the door'.

Answer: She met him at the front door.

(xix)Question

I was getting late, so we set off _____ home.

  • Direction

    📖 2.15–2.16
  1. A destination after 'set off': for.

Answer: I was getting late, so we set off for home.
