1QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

None of the answers _____ correct.

  1. (a) was ✓
  2. (b) were being
  3. (c) have
  4. (d) are being

Answer: (a) None of the answers was correct.

Show the explanation
  1. "none of" + plural noun usually takes a singular verb in careful English (the plural is informal).

2QuestionSection A · 1 marksHard

The Principal and the Headmaster _____ attended the meeting.

  1. (a) has
  2. (b) have ✓
  3. (c) is
  4. (d) was

Answer: (b) The Principal and the Headmaster have attended the meeting.

Show the explanation
  1. "the" is repeated before each title, so they are two different persons: plural verb.

4QuestionSection A · 1 marksHard

Choose the pair that fills the blanks: "Neither of the plans _____ practical, and neither the manager nor his assistants _____ satisfied."

  1. (a) is … are ✓
  2. (b) are … is
  3. (c) is … is
  4. (d) are … are

Answer: (a) is … are

Show the explanation
  1. "Neither of the plans" → singular (is).
  2. "neither the manager nor his assistants" → the nearer subject "assistants" is plural (are).

7QuestionSection B · 3 marksMedium

Correct the errors:
(a) Physics are difficult for him.
(b) The scissors is blunt.
(c) Each of the players have a new bat.

Answer: (a) Physics is difficult for him. (b) The scissors are blunt. (c) Each of the players has a new bat.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) Physics is singular in use. (b) A pair noun takes a plural verb. (c) The subject "each" is singular.

8QuestionSection B · 3 marksHard

Explain the difference between "a number of" and "the number of" with one example each.

Answer: "A number of" + plural noun means "several / many" and takes a plural verb: A number of children are suffering from cold. "The number of" means "how many": its subject is "number", so it takes a singular verb: The number of sick children is increasing.

Show the explanation
  1. In "a number of children" the real subject is "children"; in "the number of children" it is "the number".
  2. Marks: 1 for each correct rule, 1 for correct examples.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: rule:9

9QuestionSection B · 3 marksHard

Fill in the blanks with the correct verb:
(a) The committee _____ (has / have) submitted its report.
(b) The committee _____ (was / were) quarrelling among themselves.
(c) Rice and curry _____ (is / are) my favourite dish.

Answer: (a) The committee has submitted its report. (b) The committee were quarrelling among themselves. (c) Rice and curry is my favourite dish.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) "its report" shows the committee acting as one body: singular.
  2. (b) "among themselves" shows the members acting separately: plural.
  3. (c) One dish: singular.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: rule:4

10QuestionSection B · 3 marksMedium

Fill in the blanks with suitable forms of the verbs in brackets:
(a) What you say _____ (sound) reasonable.
(b) Rabi, together with his sisters, _____ (be) coming to the party.
(c) It is he who _____ (be) to blame.

Answer: (a) What you say sounds reasonable. (b) Rabi, together with his sisters, is coming to the party. (c) It is he who is to blame.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) A clause as subject takes a singular verb.
  2. (b) With "together with", the verb agrees with the first word, "Rabi".
  3. (c) The verb in the relative clause agrees with the antecedent "he".
← Back to the question 📖 Section: rule:11

11QuestionSection C · 4 marksHard

Correct the eight errors of agreement in this passage (½ mark each):
The news are good. A lot of people has come to the fair. Neither the shopkeepers nor the owner are happy with the arrangements. The scenery around the fair ground are lovely. Every one of the stalls sell sweets. Fifty rupees are the entry fee. The poor gets free entry. The United States have sent a team too.

Answer: The news is good. A lot of people have come to the fair. Neither the shopkeepers nor the owner is happy with the arrangements. The scenery around the fair ground is lovely. Every one of the stalls sells sweets. Fifty rupees is the entry fee. The poor get free entry. The United States has sent a team too.

Show the explanation
  1. "news", "scenery" → singular; "a lot of people" → plural; with neither … nor the nearer subject "the owner" → singular.
  2. "Every one of" → singular; an amount of money as one sum → singular; "the poor" (a class) → plural; the name of one country → singular.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: rule:6

12QuestionSection C · 4 marksMedium

Write four sentences of your own: (a) a collective noun acting as one body, (b) the same collective noun with its members acting separately, (c) a sentence with "a pair of", (d) a sentence with "either of".

Answer: Sample: (a) The team has won the cup. (b) The team are arguing among themselves about the captain. (c) A pair of new shoes was bought for me. (d) Either of the two roads leads to the station.

Show the explanation
  1. One mark for each sentence with the right verb: singular in (a), (c), (d); plural in (b).

13QuestionSection C · 4 marksHard

Choose the correct verb and give the reason:
(a) The teacher, as well as the pupils, (was / were) present.
(b) Three-quarters of the students (has / have) passed.
(c) Some of the milk (has / have) turned sour.
(d) The blind (need / needs) our help.

Answer: (a) was (b) have (c) has (d) need

Show the explanation
  1. (a) With "as well as", the verb agrees with the first word, "the teacher".
  2. (b) After a fraction the verb agrees with "students" (plural). (c) After "some of" it agrees with "milk" (uncountable, singular).
  3. (d) "the blind" means blind people as a class: plural.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: rule:12
