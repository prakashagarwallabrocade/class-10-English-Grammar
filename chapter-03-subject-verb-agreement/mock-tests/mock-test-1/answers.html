1QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

The children _____ playing in the park.

  1. (a) is
  2. (b) are ✓
  3. (c) was
  4. (d) has

Answer: (b) The children are playing in the park.

Show the explanation
  1. "children" is plural, so the plural auxiliary "are" is needed.

2QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

There _____ a lot of sugar in the jar.

  1. (a) is ✓
  2. (b) are
  3. (c) were
  4. (d) have

Answer: (a) There is a lot of sugar in the jar.

Show the explanation
  1. After "there" the verb agrees with the real subject; after "a lot of" that is "sugar", which is uncountable (singular).
← Back to the question 📖 Section: rule:1

3QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

Mathematics _____ my favourite subject.

  1. (a) is ✓
  2. (b) are
  3. (c) were
  4. (d) have been

Answer: (a) Mathematics is my favourite subject.

Show the explanation
  1. Names of subjects ending in -ics (Mathematics, Physics, Economics) take a singular verb.

The police _____ arrested the thief.

  3. (c) is
  4. (d) was

Answer: (b) The police have arrested the thief.

Show the explanation
  1. "police" always takes a plural verb.

Answer: (b) Each of the girls gets a prize.

  1. The subject is "each", which is singular: gets.

6QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

Ten kilometres _____ not a long way to walk.

  1. (a) is ✓
  2. (b) are
  3. (c) were
  4. (d) have

Answer: (a) Ten kilometres is not a long way to walk.

  1. A distance taken as one figure is treated as singular.

7QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

The furniture in this room _____ very old.

  1. (a) is ✓
  2. (b) are
  3. (c) were
  4. (d) have

Answer: (a) The furniture in this room is very old.

Show the explanation
  1. "furniture" is uncountable and takes a singular verb; "in this room" does not change the subject.

8QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

Neither the teacher nor the students _____ present.

  1. (a) was
  2. (b) were ✓
  3. (c) is
  4. (d) has

Answer: (b) Neither the teacher nor the students were present.

Show the explanation
  1. With neither … nor the verb agrees with the nearer subject, "the students" (plural).

9QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

The number of road accidents _____ rising every year.

  1. (a) is ✓
  2. (b) are
  3. (c) were
  4. (d) have

Answer: (a) The number of road accidents is rising every year.

Show the explanation
  1. The real subject is "the number", which is singular. ("A number of accidents are …" would be plural.)

10QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

Bread and butter _____ his usual breakfast.

  1. (a) is ✓
  2. (b) are
  3. (c) were
  4. (d) have

Answer: (a) Bread and butter is his usual breakfast.

Show the explanation
  1. Two nouns joined by "and" that make one dish (one notion) take a singular verb.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: rule:3

11QuestionSection B · 2 marksEasy

Choose the correct verb:
(a) My trousers (is / are) new.
(b) A pair of shoes (was / were) under the bed.

Answer: (a) My trousers are new. (b) A pair of shoes was under the bed.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) "trousers" is a pair noun: plural verb.
  2. (b) "a pair of …" is singular.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: rule:13

12QuestionSection B · 2 marksEasy

Fill in the blanks with is or are:
(a) The rich _____ not always happy.
(b) Measles _____ a dangerous disease.

Answer: (a) The rich are not always happy. (b) Measles is a dangerous disease.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) "the rich" means rich people as a class: plural.
  2. (b) "measles" is the name of one disease: singular.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: rule:14

13QuestionSection B · 2 marksMedium

Correct the verbs:
(a) One of my friends live in Delhi.
(b) The teacher with his students are going on a picnic.

Answer: (a) One of my friends lives in Delhi. (b) The teacher with his students is going on a picnic.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) The subject is "one" (singular).
  2. (b) With "with", the verb agrees with the first word, "the teacher".
← Back to the question 📖 Section: rule:7

14QuestionSection B · 2 marksEasy

Fill in the blanks with was or were:
(a) A herd of cows _____ grazing by the river.
(b) Some of the mangoes _____ rotten.

Answer: (a) A herd of cows was grazing by the river. (b) Some of the mangoes were rotten.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) "a herd of" takes a singular verb.
  2. (b) After "some of" the verb agrees with "mangoes" (plural).
← Back to the question 📖 Section: rule:9

15QuestionSection B · 2 marksMedium

Choose the correct verb and give the reason:
(a) It is I who (am / is) to blame.
(b) The Secretary and Treasurer of the club (has / have) arrived. (one person holds both posts)

Answer: (a) It is I who am to blame. (b) The Secretary and Treasurer of the club has arrived.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) The verb in the relative clause agrees with the antecedent "I".
  2. (b) Both titles belong to one person (only one "The"), so the verb is singular.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: rule:16

16QuestionSection C · 5 marksEasy

Fill in the blanks with suitable forms of the verbs in brackets:
Our school library _____ (be) open from 9 to 5. Most of the books _____ (be) in English. The news of the day _____ (be) put up on the notice board. A number of students _____ (visit) the library every day. Physics _____ (be) the most popular subject among them.

Answer: Our school library is open from 9 to 5. Most of the books are in English. The news of the day is put up on the notice board. A number of students visit the library every day. Physics is the most popular subject among them.

Show the explanation
  1. "library" is singular → is; after "most of" the verb agrees with "books" → are.
  2. "news" and "Physics" are singular → is.
  3. "a number of students" is plural → visit.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: rule:6

17QuestionSection C · 5 marksMedium

Rewrite the sentences choosing the correct form:
(a) Either you or your brother (has / have) broken the glass.
(b) Two-thirds of the land (is / are) desert.
(c) The jury (was / were) divided in their opinions.
(d) Gulliver’s Travels (is / are) a famous book.
(e) No students (was / were) late today.

Answer: (a) Either you or your brother has broken the glass. (b) Two-thirds of the land is desert. (c) The jury were divided in their opinions. (d) Gulliver’s Travels is a famous book. (e) No students were late today.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) The verb agrees with the nearer subject, "your brother".
  2. (b) After a fraction the verb agrees with "land" (singular).
  3. (c) "divided in their opinions" shows the members acting separately: plural.
  4. (d) The title of one book is singular. (e) "no" + plural noun → plural verb.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: rule:12
