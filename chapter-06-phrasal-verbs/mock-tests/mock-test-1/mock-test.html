Mock Test - 1

Full marks : 30Time : 45 minutes

  1. Answer all the questions. The marks for each question are shown on the right.
  2. Section A: 10 questions, 1 mark each · Section B: 5 questions, 2 marks each · Section C: 2 questions, 5 marks each.
  3. In Section A choose the correct option; in Sections B and C write complete sentences.

ASection A : Multiple Choice Questions10 × 1 = 10

  1. The meeting was _____ because of the rain.

    1. (a) called off
    2. (b) called on
    3. (c) called up
    4. (d) called for
    [1] Answer →

  2. It is dark in here. Please _____ the light.

    1. (a) turn on
    2. (b) turn down
    3. (c) turn off
    4. (d) turn out
    [1] Answer →

  3. She _____ her little brother when her mother is away.

    1. (a) looks after
    2. (b) looks for
    3. (c) looks into
    4. (d) looks up
    [1] Answer →

  4. The plane _____ at 5 p.m.

    1. (a) took off
    2. (b) took after
    3. (c) took over
    4. (d) took down
    [1] Answer →

  5. In "They have put off the meeting", "put off" means:

    1. (a) postponed
    2. (b) extinguished
    3. (c) suppressed
    4. (d) accommodated
    [1] Answer →

  6. Choose the correct sentence.

    1. (a) He gave up it.
    2. (b) He gave it up.
    3. (c) He up gave it.
    4. (d) He gave it upon.
    [1] Answer →

  7. We have _____ sugar; please buy some.

    1. (a) run out of
    2. (b) run into
    3. (c) run over
    4. (d) run down
    [1] Answer →

  8. In which sentence is the phrasal verb intransitive?

    1. (a) My car broke down.
    2. (b) They set up a school.
    3. (c) Please turn on the fan.
    4. (d) He looked up the word.
    [1] Answer →

  9. The police are _____ the matter.

    1. (a) looking into
    2. (b) looking after
    3. (c) looking up
    4. (d) looking on
    [1] Answer →

  10. Rina _____ her mother; both have curly hair.

    1. (a) takes after
    2. (b) takes off
    3. (c) takes over
    4. (d) takes in
    [1] Answer →
