(i)Question

(To replace some lost thing) We must work hard to _____ up the lost hours.

Rules used

  • Meaning of phrasal verbs

    Many phrasal verbs have a new meaning that cannot be guessed from their parts, and can be replaced by one ordinary verb (turn down = reject, fall out = quarrel).

    📖 Phrasal Verbs : Their Meanings
  1. make up = replace (supply a deficiency).

Answer: We must work hard to make up the lost hours.
