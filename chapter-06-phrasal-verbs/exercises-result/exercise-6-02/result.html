1Question

Can you pick out the correct answers ?

Rules used

  • Pronoun objects

    A pronoun object of a separable phrasal verb goes between the verb and the particle: pick them out, not pick out them.

    📖 Transitive and Intransitive use
  1. The noun phrase becomes a pronoun (it / them) and moves between the verb and the particle.

Answer: Can you pick them out ?
