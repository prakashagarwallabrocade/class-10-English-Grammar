1Question

I _____ on my way to school. (came across / a strange animal)

Rules used

  • Separable and non-separable

    With an adverb particle (blow up, take off, put down, call off) a noun object can go before or after the particle. With a prepositional verb (look for, look into, call on, count on, hit on) the object always follows the preposition: not separable (NS).

    📖 Phrasal Verbs (introduction) and the Notes to teachers
  1. 'come across' (= meet by chance) is a prepositional verb: the object follows it.

Answer: I came across a strange animal on my way to school.

2Question

It is better to _____. (throw away / broken chairs)

Rules used (as above)
  • Separable and non-separable

    With an adverb particle (blow up, take off, put down, call off) a noun object can go before or after the particle. With a prepositional verb (look for, look into, call on, count on, hit on) the object always follows the preposition: not separable (NS).

    📖 Phrasal Verbs (introduction) and the Notes to teachers
  1. A noun object may go between or after: 'throw away broken chairs' is also correct.

Answer: It is better to throw broken chairs away.

3Question

It was a mistake. My friend _____. (point out / it)

Rules used

  • Pronoun objects

    A pronoun object of a separable phrasal verb goes between the verb and the particle: pick them out, not pick out them.

    📖 Transitive and Intransitive use
  1. A pronoun object goes between verb and particle; past tense: pointed.

Answer: It was a mistake. My friend pointed it out.

5Question

9Question

This school is running well. We will _____. (close down / it)

Rules used (as above)
  • Pronoun objects

    A pronoun object of a separable phrasal verb goes between the verb and the particle: pick them out, not pick out them.

    📖 Transitive and Intransitive use
Note: The sense ('running well') calls for the negative: We will not close it down. The pattern asked for is 'close it down'.
  1. Pronoun object between verb and particle.

Answer: This school is running well. We will close it down.
