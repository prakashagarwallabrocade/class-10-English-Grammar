1Question

When the whole class was worried how to solve the problem, Suresh ____ a solution.

Rules used

  • Common phrasal verbs

    The chapter’s list gives the meaning and an example for each phrasal verb.

Answer: When the whole class was worried how to solve the problem, Suresh came out with a solution.
