(a)Question

I enjoy _____ (play) on the river bank.

Rules used

  • verb-ing

    After enjoy, mind, stop, avoid (and 'hate' / 'prefer' for a general habit) the non-finite verb takes -ing. remember / forget + to + verb = something still to do; remember / forget + V-ing = something already done.

    📖 E. (beyond the book’s list)
  1. 'enjoy' is followed by verb-ing.

Answer: I enjoy playing on the river bank.

(b)Question

Where do you want _____ (go) ?

Rules used

  • to + verb

    After verbs like agree, decide, expect, hope, learn, plan, want, wish the non-finite verb is to + verb. advise, allow, ask, persuade, remind, tell, want take a noun phrase before to + verb (He requested me to help him).

    📖 E. The non-finite verbs after the following ones are usually to + verb
  1. 'want' + to + verb.

Answer: Where do you want to go ?

(c)Question

I learnt _____ (swim) when I was eight years old.

Rules used (as above)
  • to + verb

    After verbs like agree, decide, expect, hope, learn, plan, want, wish the non-finite verb is to + verb. advise, allow, ask, persuade, remind, tell, want take a noun phrase before to + verb (He requested me to help him).

    📖 E. The non-finite verbs after the following ones are usually to + verb
  1. 'learn' + to + verb.

Answer: I learnt to swim when I was eight years old.

(d)Question

I am trying _____ (study). Please stop _____ (talk).

Rules used (as above)
  • to + verb

    After verbs like agree, decide, expect, hope, learn, plan, want, wish the non-finite verb is to + verb. advise, allow, ask, persuade, remind, tell, want take a noun phrase before to + verb (He requested me to help him).

    📖 E. The non-finite verbs after the following ones are usually to + verb
  • verb-ing

    After enjoy, mind, stop, avoid (and 'hate' / 'prefer' for a general habit) the non-finite verb takes -ing. remember / forget + to + verb = something still to do; remember / forget + V-ing = something already done.

    📖 E. (beyond the book’s list)
  1. 'try' + to + verb; 'stop' + verb-ing (stop an activity).

Answer: I am trying to study. Please stop talking.

(e)Question

I don’t mind _____ (travel) by bus but I prefer _____ (travel) by train.

Rules used (as above)
  • verb-ing

    After enjoy, mind, stop, avoid (and 'hate' / 'prefer' for a general habit) the non-finite verb takes -ing. remember / forget + to + verb = something still to do; remember / forget + V-ing = something already done.

    📖 E. (beyond the book’s list)
  • to + verb

    After verbs like agree, decide, expect, hope, learn, plan, want, wish the non-finite verb is to + verb. advise, allow, ask, persuade, remind, tell, want take a noun phrase before to + verb (He requested me to help him).

    📖 E. The non-finite verbs after the following ones are usually to + verb
  1. 'mind' + verb-ing; 'prefer' + to + verb (travelling is also correct).

Answer: I don’t mind travelling by bus but I prefer to travel by train.

(f)Question

She would not let me _____ (read) the letter.

Rules used

  • φ + verb (bare infinitive)

    After modals and after let, make, help, have, see, hear, watch, notice — and after had better / would rather — the verb comes without 'to'.

    📖 D. Sometimes to + verb may reduce to φ + verb
  1. 'let' + noun phrase + φ verb.

Answer: She would not let me read the letter.

(g)Question

Good bye. I hope _____ (see) you soon.

Rules used (as above)
  • to + verb

    After verbs like agree, decide, expect, hope, learn, plan, want, wish the non-finite verb is to + verb. advise, allow, ask, persuade, remind, tell, want take a noun phrase before to + verb (He requested me to help him).

    📖 E. The non-finite verbs after the following ones are usually to + verb
  1. 'hope' + to + verb.

Answer: Good bye. I hope to see you soon.
