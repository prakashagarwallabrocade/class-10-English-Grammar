(a)Question

She plans to buy some presents for her sister.

Rules used

  • Functions of to + verb

    Subject (To err is human), object (I hope to see you), describing a noun (a house to let), or adjunct of purpose (He let out his house to make money). After 'be' it is a complement (He is to go abroad).

    📖 C. To + verb can work in several ways
  1. She plans what? — to buy some presents: the object of 'plans'.

Answer: to buy some presents for her sister — object
