(a)Question

Please don’t tell anyone that I have been punished.
(I / not / want / anyone / know)
Ans. I don’t want anyone to know about it.

Rules used

  • to + verb

    After verbs like agree, decide, expect, hope, learn, plan, want, wish the non-finite verb is to + verb. advise, allow, ask, persuade, remind, tell, want take a noun phrase before to + verb (He requested me to help him).

    📖 E. The non-finite verbs after the following ones are usually to + verb
Note: This item is the book’s worked example.
  1. Done in the book as the example: want + anyone + to + verb.

Answer: I don’t want anyone to know about it.
