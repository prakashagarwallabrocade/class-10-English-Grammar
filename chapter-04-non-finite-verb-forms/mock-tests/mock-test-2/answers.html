1QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

I would rather _____ at home today.

  1. (a) stay ✓
  2. (b) to stay
  3. (c) staying
  4. (d) stayed

Answer: (a) I would rather stay at home today.

Show the explanation
  1. "would rather" is followed by φ + verb.

2QuestionSection A · 1 marksHard

Choose the correct sentence.

  1. (a) She let me to use her phone.
  2. (b) She allowed me use her phone.
  3. (c) She let me use her phone. ✓
  4. (d) She allowed me using her phone.

Answer: (c) She let me use her phone.

Show the explanation
  1. "let" takes φ + verb; "allow" would need "to use". Each wrong option mixes the two patterns.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: D

3QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

In "They must have been working", the non-finite verbs are:

  1. (a) must have
  2. (b) have been working ✓
  3. (c) must
  4. (d) working only

Answer: (b) have been working

Show the explanation
  1. Only the first auxiliary "must" is finite; everything after it is non-finite.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: A

4QuestionSection A · 1 marksHard

Don’t forget _____ the lights before you leave.

  1. (a) to switch off ✓
  2. (b) switching off
  3. (c) switch off
  4. (d) switched off

Answer: (a) Don’t forget to switch off the lights before you leave.

Show the explanation
  1. forget + to + verb refers to something still to be done; "forget switching off" would refer to a memory of something done.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: E

5QuestionSection A · 1 marksHard

I remember _____ him at the fair last year.

  1. (a) to meet
  2. (b) meeting ✓
  3. (c) meet
  4. (d) met

Answer: (b) I remember meeting him at the fair last year.

Show the explanation
  1. "last year" shows the meeting already happened: remember + -ing = a memory of a past action.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: E

6QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

In "There is a house to let at the end of the road", the to + verb:

  1. (a) works as the subject
  2. (b) works as the object
  3. (c) describes a noun ✓
  4. (d) is an adjunct of purpose

Answer: (c) describes a noun

Show the explanation
  1. "to let" tells us about the head noun "house", like an adjective.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: C

7QuestionSection B · 3 marksHard

Rewrite each sentence using a non-finite verb and the word in brackets:
(a) "Don’t make a noise," the teacher said to us. (told)
(b) Rita said that I could use her pen. (allowed)
(c) I am sure Rahim will help me. (expect)

Answer: (a) The teacher told us not to make a noise. (b) Rita allowed me to use her pen. (c) I expect Rahim to help me.

Show the explanation
  1. tell / allow / expect + noun phrase + to + verb; a negative instruction becomes "not to + verb".
← Back to the question 📖 Section: E

8QuestionSection B · 3 marksMedium

Fill in the blanks with to + verb, φ + verb or verb-ing:
(a) Would you mind _____ (open) the window?
(b) The police made him _____ (confess) his crime.
(c) We plan _____ (visit) Konark next month.

Answer: (a) Would you mind opening the window? (b) The police made him confess his crime. (c) We plan to visit Konark next month.

Show the explanation
  1. "mind" + -ing; "make" + object + φ verb; "plan" + to + verb.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: E

9QuestionSection B · 3 marksHard

Break each sentence into two simple sentences:
(a) I saw the thief run away.
(b) They expected me to win.
(c) We heard the baby cry.

Answer: (a) The thief ran away. + I saw it. (b) I would win. + They expected it. (c) The baby cried. + We heard it.

Show the explanation
  1. The noun phrase before the non-finite verb is the subject of the hidden clause; the main clause takes "it" as its object.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: B

10QuestionSection B · 3 marksHard

Explain the difference in meaning:
(a) He remembered to lock the door. / He remembered locking the door.
(b) She forgot to post the letter. / She will never forget posting that letter.

Answer: (a) The first means he did not forget to do it (he locked it); the second means he has a memory of having locked it. (b) The first means the letter was not posted; the second is a memory of an action already done.

Show the explanation
  1. remember / forget + to + verb = an action still to be done; + verb-ing = an action already done, remembered (or forgotten).
← Back to the question 📖 Section: E

11QuestionSection C · 4 marksHard

Fill in the blanks with suitable non-finite forms of the verbs in brackets (½ mark each):
Ravi decided _____ (learn) swimming. His father agreed _____ (pay) the fee. The coach made him _____ (practise) every morning. At first Ravi was afraid _____ (enter) the deep water, but soon he enjoyed _____ (float) on it. Within a month he could _____ (swim) across the pool. His father was happy _____ (see) his progress. Now Ravi hopes _____ (take) part in a competition.

Answer: Ravi decided to learn swimming. His father agreed to pay the fee. The coach made him practise every morning. At first Ravi was afraid to enter the deep water, but soon he enjoyed floating on it. Within a month he could swim across the pool. His father was happy to see his progress. Now Ravi hopes to take part in a competition.

Show the explanation
  1. decide, agree, hope → to + verb; afraid / happy + to + verb.
  2. make (+ object) and the modal could → φ + verb; enjoy → -ing.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: E

12QuestionSection C · 4 marksMedium

Write one sentence of your own with each of these: (a) let + φ verb (b) want + noun phrase + to + verb (c) enjoy + verb-ing (d) had better + φ verb.

Answer: Sample: (a) My mother lets me watch TV on Sundays. (b) I want my brother to join the football team. (c) We enjoy reading comics. (d) You had better take an umbrella.

Show the explanation
  1. One mark for each sentence with the right non-finite form.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: D

13QuestionSection C · 4 marksHard

Correct the errors:
(a) My mother let me to go to the fair.
(b) He avoided to answer my question.
(c) We heard him to sing a song.
(d) She refused helping me.

Answer: (a) My mother let me go to the fair. (b) He avoided answering my question. (c) We heard him sing a song. (d) She refused to help me.

Show the explanation
  1. "let" and "hear" + object + φ verb; "avoid" + -ing; "refuse" + to + verb.
