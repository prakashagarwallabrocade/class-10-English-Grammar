BSection B : Short Answer Questions4 × 3 = 12

  1. Rewrite each sentence using a non-finite verb and the word in brackets:
    (a) "Don’t make a noise," the teacher said to us. (told)
    (b) Rita said that I could use her pen. (allowed)
    (c) I am sure Rahim will help me. (expect)

    [3] Answer →

  2. Fill in the blanks with to + verb, φ + verb or verb-ing:
    (a) Would you mind _____ (open) the window?
    (b) The police made him _____ (confess) his crime.
    (c) We plan _____ (visit) Konark next month.

    [3] Answer →

  3. Break each sentence into two simple sentences:
    (a) I saw the thief run away.
    (b) They expected me to win.
    (c) We heard the baby cry.

    [3] Answer →

  4. Explain the difference in meaning:
    (a) He remembered to lock the door. / He remembered locking the door.
    (b) She forgot to post the letter. / She will never forget posting that letter.

    [3] Answer →
