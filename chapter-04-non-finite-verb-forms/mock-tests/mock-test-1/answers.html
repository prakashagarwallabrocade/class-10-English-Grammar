1QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

In "She must have left", the finite verb is:

  1. (a) must ✓
  2. (b) have
  3. (c) left
  4. (d) have left

Answer: (a) must

Show the explanation
  1. Only the first word of the verb group (the modal "must") is finite; "have left" is non-finite.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: A

2QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

Let me _____ your book.

  1. (a) see ✓
  2. (b) to see
  3. (c) seeing
  4. (d) saw

Answer: (a) Let me see your book.

Show the explanation
  1. "let" is followed by a noun phrase + φ verb (no "to").

3QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

I want _____ a doctor.

  1. (a) become
  2. (b) to become ✓
  3. (c) becoming
  4. (d) became

Answer: (b) I want to become a doctor.

Show the explanation
  1. "want" is followed by to + verb.

4QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

He enjoys _____ cricket.

  1. (a) play
  2. (b) to play
  3. (c) playing ✓
  4. (d) played

Answer: (c) He enjoys playing cricket.

Show the explanation
  1. "enjoy" is followed by the -ing form.

5QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

The teacher made the boy _____ the answer again.

  1. (a) write ✓
  2. (b) to write
  3. (c) writing
  4. (d) written

Answer: (a) The teacher made the boy write the answer again.

Show the explanation
  1. "make" + object + φ verb: made the boy write (not "to write").

6QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

We decided _____ the meeting.

  1. (a) attend
  2. (b) to attend ✓
  3. (c) attending
  4. (d) attended

Answer: (b) We decided to attend the meeting.

Show the explanation
  1. "decide" is followed by to + verb.

7QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

Which of these is NOT a correct sentence, because it has no finite verb?

  1. (a) She is writing a letter.
  2. (b) She writing a letter. ✓
  3. (c) She writes letters.
  4. (d) She has written a letter.

Answer: (b) She writing a letter.

Show the explanation
  1. "writing" is non-finite; with no finite verb (is / was) the group of words is not a sentence.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: A

8QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

In "To err is human", the to + verb works as the:

  1. (a) subject ✓
  2. (b) object
  3. (c) complement
  4. (d) adjunct

Answer: (a) subject

Show the explanation
  1. What is human? — "To err": it is the subject of "is".
← Back to the question 📖 Section: C

9QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

You had better _____ now.

  1. (a) go ✓
  2. (b) to go
  3. (c) going
  4. (d) gone

Answer: (a) You had better go now.

Show the explanation
  1. "had better" is followed by φ + verb.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: D

10QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

He went to the market _____ vegetables.

  1. (a) buy
  2. (b) to buy ✓
  3. (c) buying
  4. (d) bought

Answer: (b) He went to the market to buy vegetables.

Show the explanation
  1. The purpose of going is shown by to + verb (an adjunct).

11QuestionSection B · 2 marksEasy

Fill in the blanks with the right form of the verbs in brackets:
(a) I heard her _____ (sing) a song.
(b) Please help me _____ (carry) this box.

Answer: (a) I heard her sing a song. (b) Please help me carry this box.

Show the explanation
  1. "hear" and "help" + noun phrase + φ verb.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: D

12QuestionSection B · 2 marksEasy

Pick out the non-finite verbs:
(a) He has been sleeping.
(b) They will have gone.

Answer: (a) been, sleeping (b) have, gone

Show the explanation
  1. The first auxiliaries "has" and "will" are finite; the rest of each verb group is non-finite.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: A

13QuestionSection B · 2 marksMedium

Break each sentence into two simple sentences:
(a) I want you to come early.
(b) The teacher let us go home.

Answer: (a) You will come early. + I want it. (b) We went home. + The teacher let us (do it).

Show the explanation
  1. The noun phrase before the non-finite verb ("you", "us") becomes the subject of the hidden clause.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: B

14QuestionSection B · 2 marksEasy

Fill in the blanks with to + verb or verb-ing:
(a) I hope _____ (pass) the test.
(b) Stop _____ (shout).

Answer: (a) I hope to pass the test. (b) Stop shouting.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) "hope" + to + verb. (b) "stop" (an activity) + verb-ing.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: E

15QuestionSection B · 2 marksMedium

Correct these sentences:
(a) He made me to cry.
(b) I want that you help me.

Answer: (a) He made me cry. (b) I want you to help me.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) "make" + object + φ verb (no "to").
  2. (b) "want" + noun phrase + to + verb, not a that-clause.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: D

16QuestionSection C · 5 marksEasy

Fill in the blanks with to + verb or φ + verb:
My mother asked me _____ (buy) some milk. On the way I saw a man _____ (fall) off his bicycle. I helped him _____ (stand) up. He thanked me and wanted _____ (give) me some money, but I refused _____ (take) it.

Answer: My mother asked me to buy some milk. On the way I saw a man fall off his bicycle. I helped him stand up. He thanked me and wanted to give me some money, but I refused to take it.

Show the explanation
  1. "ask", "want" and "refuse" take to + verb.
  2. "see" and "help" + noun phrase take φ + verb.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: E

17QuestionSection C · 5 marksMedium

Say how the to + verb works in each sentence (subject, object, complement, adjunct, or describing a noun):
(a) To swim in this river is dangerous.
(b) She likes to sing.
(c) He saved money to buy a bicycle.
(d) There is no water to drink.
(e) Our aim is to win.

Answer: (a) subject (b) object (c) adjunct (d) describing a noun (e) complement

Show the explanation
  1. (a) What is dangerous? (b) She likes what? (c) Why did he save money? — purpose.
  2. (d) "to drink" describes "water". (e) It completes "is": a complement.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: C
