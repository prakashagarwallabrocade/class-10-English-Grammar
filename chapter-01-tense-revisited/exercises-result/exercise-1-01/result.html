1Question

He ——— (get) up early everyday. He —— (brush) his teeth and ——— (have) his bath. He —— ( take) a quick breakfast. He —— (not want) to be punished at school. So, he —— (set out) early to reach school in time. He —— (sit) on the same bench in my classroom. He —— (have) straight hair and a sharp nose. What is his name?

Rules used

  • Present simple

    Habits, laws of nature, general or scientific truths and permanent states or facts: V1, with -s/-es after he/she/it. Verbs of state (like, want, prefer, taste, know) normally stay in the simple form even for 'now'.

    📖 Ways of expressing the present time, 1
Note: The last question is a riddle: the answer is the name of the classmate you described. With 'she' the verbs stay the same; only the pronouns change.
  1. The passage describes what the boy does every day, so all the verbs are in the present simple.
  2. The subject is 'he' (third person singular), so each verb takes -s or -es: get → gets, brush → brushes, have → has, take → takes, set out → sets out, sit → sits.
  3. The negative is formed with does not + V1: does not want (not 'does not wants').

Answer: He gets up early everyday. He brushes his teeth and has his bath. He takes a quick breakfast. He does not want to be punished at school. So, he sets out early to reach school in time. He sits on the same bench in my classroom. He has straight hair and a sharp nose. What is his name?
