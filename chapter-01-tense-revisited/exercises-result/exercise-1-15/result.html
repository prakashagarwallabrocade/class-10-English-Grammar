1Question

Federer : My best moment in the match was when I realized that the game (finish) and I (become) the champion. I (win) at last. At that moment I (become) the best Tennis player of the world.

Rules used

  • Past perfect

    The older of two past actions: had + V3. The later one is in the past simple.

    📖 Ways of expressing the past time, 4
  1. All these actions were complete before the moment he realized it, so they take the past perfect.

Answer: Federer : My best moment in the match was when I realized that the game had finished and I had become the champion. I had won at last. At that moment I had become the best Tennis player of the world.

2Question

Reporter : And when you won, how long (you / play) Tennis?

Rules used

  • Past perfect progressive

    A past action that had been going on for some time before another past action: had been + V-ing.

    📖 Ways of expressing the past time, 6
  1. 'how long … when you won' — an activity going on up to a past moment: past perfect progressive.

Answer: Reporter : And when you won, how long had you been playing Tennis?

3Question

Federer : I started just before I finished school. That means, I (play) for about six years when I won the Wimbledon.

Rules used (as above)
  • Past perfect progressive

    A past action that had been going on for some time before another past action: had been + V-ing.

    📖 Ways of expressing the past time, 6
  1. 'for about six years' up to the win: past perfect progressive.

Answer: Federer : I started just before I finished school. That means, I had been playing for about six years when I won the Wimbledon.

4Question

Reporter : You (beat) some good players before you won that tournament.

Rules used (as above)
  • Past perfect

    The older of two past actions: had + V3. The later one is in the past simple.

    📖 Ways of expressing the past time, 4
  1. Completed actions before another past action: past perfect.

Answer: Reporter : You had beaten some good players before you won that tournament.

5Question

Federer : Yes. Ever since I started as a Tennis professional, I (win) games regularly, until my injury.

Rules used (as above)
  • Past perfect progressive

    A past action that had been going on for some time before another past action: had been + V-ing.

    📖 Ways of expressing the past time, 6
  1. 'Ever since … until my injury' — a repeated activity continuing up to a past point: past perfect progressive.

Answer: Federer : Yes. Ever since I started as a Tennis professional, I had been winning games regularly, until my injury.
