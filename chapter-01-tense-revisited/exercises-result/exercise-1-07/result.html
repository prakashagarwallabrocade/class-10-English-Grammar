1Question

Ashok : What are these people doing here? What (happen)?

Rules used

  • Present perfect

    A past action whose result matters now (just, already, yet, by now, so far, this week): has/have + V3.

    📖 Ways of expressing the past time, 1
  1. He sees the result now and asks about it: present perfect.

Answer: Ashok : What are these people doing here? What has happened?

2Question

Bini : There (be) an accident.

Rules used (as above)
  • Present perfect

    A past action whose result matters now (just, already, yet, by now, so far, this week): has/have + V3.

    📖 Ways of expressing the past time, 1
  1. News of a recent event that matters now: present perfect.

Answer: Bini : There has been an accident.

3Question

Ashok : An accident? What (happen) exactly?

Rules used

  • Past simple

    A finished action at a past time (last night, ago, when …?), or one event after another in a story: V2.

    📖 Ways of expressing the past time, 3
  1. Now he asks for the details of a finished event: past simple.

Answer: Ashok : An accident? What happened exactly?

4Question

Bini : A cat (run) across the road in front of a car. The driver (try) to apply the brake and (hit) the tree on the side of the road.

Rules used (as above)
  • Past simple

    A finished action at a past time (last night, ago, when …?), or one event after another in a story: V2.

    📖 Ways of expressing the past time, 3
  1. A story of finished actions one after another: past simple (run → ran, try → tried, hit → hit).

Answer: Bini : A cat ran across the road in front of a car. The driver tried to apply the brake and hit the tree on the side of the road.

5Question

Ashok : When (it happen)?

Rules used (as above)
  • Past simple

    A finished action at a past time (last night, ago, when …?), or one event after another in a story: V2.

    📖 Ways of expressing the past time, 3
  1. A question with 'when' asks for a past time, so it takes the past simple (did + V1), never the present perfect.

Answer: Ashok : When did it happen?

6Question

Bini : About ten minutes ago.

  1. No verb in brackets; 'ago' itself is a past-simple word.

Answer: Bini : About ten minutes ago.

7Question

Ashok : (anyone call) for an ambulance?

Rules used (as above)
  • Present perfect

    A past action whose result matters now (just, already, yet, by now, so far, this week): has/have + V3.

    📖 Ways of expressing the past time, 1
  1. Is the result there now? — present perfect (with 'not yet' in the reply).

Answer: Ashok : Has anyone called for an ambulance?

8Question

Bini : No, not yet. But the police (arrive). They will take care of that.

Rules used (as above)
  • Present perfect

    A past action whose result matters now (just, already, yet, by now, so far, this week): has/have + V3.

    📖 Ways of expressing the past time, 1
  1. 'not yet' and the result now (the police are here): present perfect.

Answer: Bini : No, not yet. But the police have arrived. They will take care of that.

9Question

Ashok : This is the second accident we (have) here this week.

Rules used (as above)
  • Present perfect

    A past action whose result matters now (just, already, yet, by now, so far, this week): has/have + V3.

    📖 Ways of expressing the past time, 1
  1. After 'This is the first/second … ' and with 'this week' (not finished) we use the present perfect.

Answer: Ashok : This is the second accident we have had here this week.
