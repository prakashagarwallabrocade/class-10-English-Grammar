1Question

I live in Sambalpur.

Rules used

  • Present simple

    Habits, laws of nature, general or scientific truths and permanent states or facts: V1, with -s/-es after he/she/it. Verbs of state (like, want, prefer, taste, know) normally stay in the simple form even for 'now'.

    📖 Ways of expressing the present time, 1
  1. Where I live is a lasting fact.
  2. Present simple: (a), (b), (c). Present progressive: (d), (e), (f).

Answer: (b) Permanent state or permanent fact

2Question

She’s staying with her aunt at the moment.

Rules used

  • Present progressive

    Something happening now, over a period of time (these days) or for a short time: am/is/are + V-ing.

    📖 Ways of expressing the present time, 2
  1. 'at the moment' — she is staying with her aunt only for a short time.
  2. Present simple: (a), (b), (c). Present progressive: (d), (e), (f).

Answer: (f) Temporary action
