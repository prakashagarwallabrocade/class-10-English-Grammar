1Question

Water freezes at 4°C.

Rules used

  • Present simple

    Habits, laws of nature, general or scientific truths and permanent states or facts: V1, with -s/-es after he/she/it. Verbs of state (like, want, prefer, taste, know) normally stay in the simple form even for 'now'.

    📖 Ways of expressing the present time, 1
  1. A scientific fact. Facts and truths are told in the present simple.
  2. To correct a partner, first deny with do/does not (or is not), then give the true sentence, as in the book’s example.

Answer: No, it doesn’t freeze at 4°C. Water freezes at 0°C.
