1QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

Water _____ at 100°C.

  1. (a) boil
  2. (b) boils ✓
  3. (c) is boiling
  4. (d) has boiled

Answer: (b) Water boils at 100°C.

Show the explanation
  1. A scientific truth takes the present simple; with the subject "water" (it) the verb adds -s.

2QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

Listen! Somebody _____ at the door.

  1. (a) knocks
  2. (b) is knocking ✓
  3. (c) knocked
  4. (d) has knocked

Answer: (b) Listen! Somebody is knocking at the door.

Show the explanation
  1. "Listen!" shows that the action is happening now: present progressive (is + V-ing).

3QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

She _____ her homework already, so she can watch TV now.

  1. (a) finished
  2. (b) has finished ✓
  3. (c) finishes
  4. (d) had finished

Answer: (b) She has finished her homework already, so she can watch TV now.

Show the explanation
  1. "already" and a result that matters now (she can watch TV) call for the present perfect: has + V3.

4QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

I _____ my uncle last Sunday.

  1. (a) have met
  2. (b) met ✓
  3. (c) meet
  4. (d) had met

Answer: (b) I met my uncle last Sunday.

Show the explanation
  1. "last Sunday" is a finished past time, so the past simple is used. The present perfect never goes with such a time.

5QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

When we reached the station, the train _____.

  1. (a) left
  2. (b) has left
  3. (c) had left ✓
  4. (d) leaves

Answer: (c) When we reached the station, the train had left.

Show the explanation
  1. The train left before we reached the station: the older of two past actions takes the past perfect (had + V3).
  2. "left" would mean it left at the moment we arrived.

6QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

Look at those dark clouds! It _____ rain.

  1. (a) will
  2. (b) is going to ✓
  3. (c) shall
  4. (d) is raining to

Answer: (b) Look at those dark clouds! It is going to rain.

Show the explanation
  1. We can already see the cause (dark clouds), so the future result is expressed with "be going to".

7QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

"The phone is ringing." — "I _____ it."

  1. (a) answer
  2. (b) am going to answer
  3. (c) will answer ✓
  4. (d) answered

Answer: (c) "The phone is ringing." — "I will answer it."

Show the explanation
  1. The speaker decides at the moment of speaking, so "will" is used. "am going to" would mean a decision made earlier.

8QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

Which sentence is correct?

  1. (a) I am knowing her well.
  2. (b) I know her well. ✓
  3. (c) I knowing her well.
  4. (d) I has known her well.

Answer: (b) I know her well.

Show the explanation
  1. "know" is a verb of state; it is not used in the progressive. With "I" the present simple has no -s.
  2. "I knowing" has no finite verb, and "I has" breaks subject–verb agreement.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: Ways of expressing the present time

9QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

They _____ football when it started to rain.

  1. (a) played
  2. (b) were playing ✓
  3. (c) have played
  4. (d) had played

Answer: (b) They were playing football when it started to rain.

Show the explanation
  1. The game was in progress when the rain began (a shorter action): past progressive + past simple.

10QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

According to the official schedule, the match _____ at 4.30 pm tomorrow.

  1. (a) begins ✓
  2. (b) will be begin
  3. (c) has begun
  4. (d) had begun

Answer: (a) According to the official schedule, the match begins at 4.30 pm tomorrow.

Show the explanation
  1. An official programme or timetable takes the present simple even for the future.
  2. "will be begin" is not a correct verb form.

11QuestionSection B · 2 marksEasy

Fill in the blanks with the correct forms of the verbs in brackets:
My sister _____ (teach) in a school. These days she _____ (prepare) for an examination.

Answer: My sister teaches in a school. These days she is preparing for an examination.

Show the explanation
  1. Her job is a permanent fact: present simple, "teaches" (she → -es).
  2. "These days" shows an activity going on over a period of time: present progressive, "is preparing".

12QuestionSection B · 2 marksEasy

Make a sentence with the present perfect progressive, using the verb in brackets:
(a) Rina’s eyes are red. (cry)
(b) The ground is wet. (rain)

Answer: (a) She has been crying. (b) It has been raining.

Show the explanation
  1. In both, what we see now is the effect of an activity that went on for some time: has been + V-ing.

13QuestionSection B · 2 marksMedium

Combine each pair into one sentence using the past perfect for the earlier action:
(a) He finished his work. Then he went home. (After …)
(b) The patient died. Then the doctor arrived. (When …)

Answer: (a) After he had finished his work, he went home. (b) When the doctor arrived, the patient had died.

Show the explanation
  1. Find the older action in each pair: finishing the work, and the death of the patient.
  2. The older action takes had + V3; the later one stays in the past simple.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: Ways of expressing the past time

14QuestionSection B · 2 marksEasy

Fill in the blanks with shall or will:
(a) "It is hot in here. _____ I open the window?"
(b) "Those bags look heavy. I _____ help you with them."

Answer: (a) Shall I open the window? (b) I will help you with them.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) "Shall I …?" offers to do something.
  2. (b) An offer decided on the spot: will.

15QuestionSection B · 2 marksMedium

Correct the verb in each sentence:
(a) I have seen him yesterday.
(b) She is having two brothers.

Answer: (a) I saw him yesterday. (b) She has two brothers.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) "yesterday" is a finished time, so the past simple is needed, not the present perfect.
  2. (b) "have" meaning "possess" is a verb of state; it does not take the progressive.

16QuestionSection C · 5 marksEasy

Fill in the blanks with the past simple or the past progressive forms of the verbs in brackets:
Yesterday, while I _____ (walk) home, I _____ (see) an old man at the crossing. He _____ (try) to cross the road, but the cars _____ (move) very fast. So I _____ (help) him across.

Answer: Yesterday, while I was walking home, I saw an old man at the crossing. He was trying to cross the road, but the cars were moving very fast. So I helped him across.

Show the explanation
  1. "while I was walking" — a background action in progress: past progressive.
  2. "saw" and "helped" are short completed actions in the story: past simple.
  3. "was trying" and "were moving" describe what was going on at that moment: past progressive.

17QuestionSection C · 5 marksMedium

Complete the dialogue with will, be going to or the present progressive forms of the verbs in brackets:
Ravi : What _____ (you / do) this Saturday?
Gita : I _____ (visit) my grandmother. I have already bought the bus ticket.
Ravi : That bag looks heavy. I _____ (carry) it to the bus stop for you.
Gita : Thanks. But look at the sky — it _____ (rain).
Ravi : Don’t worry. I _____ (bring) my umbrella.

Answer: Ravi : What are you doing this Saturday? Gita : I am visiting my grandmother. Ravi : I will carry it to the bus stop for you. Gita : It is going to rain. Ravi : I will bring my umbrella.

Show the explanation
  1. Plans for Saturday are arrangements (the ticket is bought): present progressive — "are you doing", "am visiting". ("am going to visit" is also accepted.)
  2. Ravi offers help on the spot: "will carry". He decides at that moment to fetch the umbrella: "will bring".
  3. The sky shows signs of rain now: "is going to rain".
