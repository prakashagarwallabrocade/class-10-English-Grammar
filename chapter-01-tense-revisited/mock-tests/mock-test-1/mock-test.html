Mock Test - 1

Full marks : 30Time : 45 minutes

  1. Answer all the questions. The marks for each question are shown on the right.
  2. Section A: 10 questions, 1 mark each · Section B: 5 questions, 2 marks each · Section C: 2 questions, 5 marks each.
  3. In Section A choose the correct option; in Sections B and C write complete sentences.

  1. Water _____ at 100°C.

    1. (a) boil
    2. (b) boils
    3. (c) is boiling
    4. (d) has boiled
    [1] Answer →

  2. Listen! Somebody _____ at the door.

    1. (a) knocks
    2. (b) is knocking
    3. (c) knocked
    4. (d) has knocked
    [1] Answer →

  3. She _____ her homework already, so she can watch TV now.

    1. (a) finished
    2. (b) has finished
    3. (c) finishes
    4. (d) had finished
    [1] Answer →

  4. I _____ my uncle last Sunday.

    1. (a) have met
    2. (b) met
    3. (c) meet
    4. (d) had met
    [1] Answer →

  5. When we reached the station, the train _____.

    1. (a) left
    2. (b) has left
    3. (c) had left
    4. (d) leaves
    [1] Answer →

  6. Look at those dark clouds! It _____ rain.

    1. (a) will
    2. (b) is going to
    3. (c) shall
    4. (d) is raining to
    [1] Answer →

  7. "The phone is ringing." — "I _____ it."

    1. (a) answer
    2. (b) am going to answer
    3. (c) will answer
    4. (d) answered
    [1] Answer →

  8. Which sentence is correct?

    1. (a) I am knowing her well.
    2. (b) I know her well.
    3. (c) I knowing her well.
    4. (d) I has known her well.
    [1] Answer →

  9. They _____ football when it started to rain.

    1. (a) played
    2. (b) were playing
    3. (c) have played
    4. (d) had played
    [1] Answer →

  10. According to the official schedule, the match _____ at 4.30 pm tomorrow.

    1. (a) begins
    2. (b) will be begin
    3. (c) has begun
    4. (d) had begun

BSection B : Short Answer Questions5 × 2 = 10

  1. Fill in the blanks with the correct forms of the verbs in brackets:
    My sister _____ (teach) in a school. These days she _____ (prepare) for an examination.

    [2] Answer →

  2. Make a sentence with the present perfect progressive, using the verb in brackets:
    (a) Rina’s eyes are red. (cry)
    (b) The ground is wet. (rain)

    [2] Answer →

  3. Combine each pair into one sentence using the past perfect for the earlier action:
    (a) He finished his work. Then he went home. (After …)
    (b) The patient died. Then the doctor arrived. (When …)

    [2] Answer →

  4. Fill in the blanks with shall or will:
    (a) "It is hot in here. _____ I open the window?"
    (b) "Those bags look heavy. I _____ help you with them."

    [2] Answer →

  5. Correct the verb in each sentence:
    (a) I have seen him yesterday.
    (b) She is having two brothers.

    [2] Answer →
