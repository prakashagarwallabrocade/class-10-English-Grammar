1Question

Train up the children in the way they should go.

Rules used

  • Kinds of adverb clauses

    Time (when, while, as, after, before, since, till, as soon as) · Place (where, wherever) · Manner (as, as if, as though) · Purpose (so that, in order that) · Reason (because, as, since) · Comparison (than, as … as) · Concession (although, though, even though, even if, whether … or not) · Condition (if, unless, provided, supposing).

    📖 Adverb Clauses (introduction)
  1. It tells how to train them.

Answer: Train up the children in the way they should go. — adverb clause of manner

2Question
