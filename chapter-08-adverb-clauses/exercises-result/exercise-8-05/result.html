iQuestion

The sun is shining and the day is quite warm.

Rules used

  • Simple, compound and complex

    Simple: one clause. Compound: two or more independent clauses (joined by and, but, so, or simply by commas). Complex: one main clause + one or more subordinate (noun, relative or adverb) clauses.

    📖 Sentence types
  1. Two independent clauses joined by 'and'.

Answer: (D) compound
