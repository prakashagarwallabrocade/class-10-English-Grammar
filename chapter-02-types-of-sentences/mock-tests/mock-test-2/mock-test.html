Mock Test - 2

Full marks : 30Time : 60 minutes

  1. Answer all the questions. The marks for each question are shown on the right.
  2. Section A: 6 questions, 1 mark each · Section B: 4 questions, 3 marks each · Section C: 3 questions, 4 marks each.
  3. In Section A choose the correct option; in Sections B and C write complete sentences.

ASection A : Multiple Choice Questions6 × 1 = 6

  1. Which of these is NOT an imperative sentence?

    1. (a) Kindly help me.
    2. (b) Never tell lies.
    3. (c) Let’s start now.
    4. (d) You are late again.
    [1] Answer →

  2. In "The teacher may have been waiting for us", the finite verb is:

    1. (a) may
    2. (b) have
    3. (c) been
    4. (d) waiting
    [1] Answer →

  3. Choose the correct sentence.

    1. (a) Neither he or his brother came.
    2. (b) Either he nor his brother came.
    3. (c) Neither he nor his brother came.
    4. (d) Both he nor his brother came.
    [1] Answer →

  4. The declarative form of "What a fool I have been!" is:

    1. (a) I have been a great fool.
    2. (b) I have not been a fool.
    3. (c) Have I been a fool?
    4. (d) Be a fool.
    [1] Answer →

  5. Which sentence is compound?

    1. (a) Although he was tired, he kept working.
    2. (b) He was tired, yet he kept working.
    3. (c) Being tired, he kept working.
    4. (d) He kept working in spite of his tiredness.
    [1] Answer →

  6. The pattern of "My father bought me a watch." is:

    1. (a) SVO
    2. (b) SVOO
    3. (c) SVC
    4. (d) SVOC
    [1] Answer →

BSection B : Short Answer Questions4 × 3 = 12

  1. Turn into exclamatory sentences:
    (a) It is a very sad story.
    (b) The child sings very sweetly.
    (c) You have grown very tall.

    [3] Answer →

  2. Combine using the correlatives given:
    (a) Ravi likes cricket. Ravi likes football. (both … and)
    (b) She did not cry. She did not complain. (neither … nor)
    (c) He is a singer. He is also a painter. (not only … but also)

    [3] Answer →

  3. Rewrite each as a complex sentence, using the word in brackets:
    (a) Hardworking students succeed. (who)
    (b) Tell me your address. (where)
    (c) I am sure of his honesty. (that)

    [3] Answer →

  4. Pick out the subordinate clause and say whether it is a noun, adverb or relative clause:

    (b) We left before the rain started.
    (c) This is the house that Jack built.
