(i)Question

Abdul is a strong boy.

Rules used

  • Four kinds of simple sentences

    Declarative: makes a statement. Interrogative: asks a question (Yes/No or Wh-). Imperative: advice, request, command, suggestion, prayer, warning, instruction, order or wish — the subject 'you' is not mentioned and the verb is in its base form. Exclamatory: shows a feeling, begins with What or How and ends with '!'.

    📖 II. Kinds of Simple Sentences
  1. It makes a statement about Abdul.

Answer: Declarative
