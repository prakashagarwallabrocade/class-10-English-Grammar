(i)Question

Nilima has a good memory.

Rules used

  • Finite verb

    The first word of the verb phrase is the finite verb: it carries the tense (it can be changed into the past) and agrees with the subject. A simple sentence has one finite clause.

    📖 Types of Sentences (introduction)
  1. 'has' is the only verb; it changes to 'had' in the past.

Answer: Finite verb: has

(ii)Question

He shall not enter the house.

Rules used (as above)
  • Finite verb

    The first word of the verb phrase is the finite verb: it carries the tense (it can be changed into the past) and agrees with the subject. A simple sentence has one finite clause.

    📖 Types of Sentences (introduction)
  1. In 'shall not enter' the first word, the modal 'shall', is finite; 'enter' is non-finite.

Answer: Finite verb: shall

(iii)Question

We have not yet received the money.

Rules used (as above)
  • Finite verb

    The first word of the verb phrase is the finite verb: it carries the tense (it can be changed into the past) and agrees with the subject. A simple sentence has one finite clause.

    📖 Types of Sentences (introduction)
  1. In 'have not yet received' the first auxiliary 'have' is finite (→ had).

Answer: Finite verb: have

(iv)Question

He has been studying to pass the examination.

Rules used (as above)
  • Finite verb

    The first word of the verb phrase is the finite verb: it carries the tense (it can be changed into the past) and agrees with the subject. A simple sentence has one finite clause.

    📖 Types of Sentences (introduction)
  1. In 'has been studying' only 'has' is finite; 'to pass' is also non-finite.

Answer: Finite verb: has

(v)Question

Ramesh works in a bank.

Rules used (as above)
  • Finite verb

    The first word of the verb phrase is the finite verb: it carries the tense (it can be changed into the past) and agrees with the subject. A simple sentence has one finite clause.

    📖 Types of Sentences (introduction)
  1. 'works' agrees with Ramesh and changes to 'worked' in the past.

Answer: Finite verb: works
