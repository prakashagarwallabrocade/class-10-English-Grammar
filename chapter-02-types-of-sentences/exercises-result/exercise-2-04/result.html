1Question

The examination is at hand. _____ the best use of your time. _____ on your studies. _____ watching television. _____ the advice of your teachers when you are in difficulty. Don’t _____ nervous. Always _____ for the best.

Rules used

  • Imperative sentences

    Begin with the verb in its base form; the subject 'you' is understood. Negative: Don’t + verb.

    📖 C. Imperative Sentences
  1. Every sentence is a piece of advice, so each blank takes a verb in its base form at the beginning (imperative).
  2. Match the meaning: make the best use, concentrate on, avoid watching, take advice, get nervous, hope for the best.

Answer: The examination is at hand. Make the best use of your time. Concentrate on your studies. Avoid watching television. Take the advice of your teachers when you are in difficulty. Don’t get nervous. Always hope for the best.
