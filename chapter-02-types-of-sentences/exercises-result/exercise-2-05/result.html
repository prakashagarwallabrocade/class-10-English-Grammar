(i)Question

He finished his exercises. He put away his books.

Rules used

  • Compound sentence

    Two or more independent clauses joined by a coordinating conjunction: and (addition), but / yet / still (contrast), or / or else / otherwise (alternative, warning), so / therefore (result), for (reason).

    📖 III. Compound Sentences
  1. One action after another: 'and'.

Answer: He finished his exercises and put away his books.

(ii)Question

He must not be late. He will be punished.

Rules used (as above)
  • Compound sentence

    Two or more independent clauses joined by a coordinating conjunction: and (addition), but / yet / still (contrast), or / or else / otherwise (alternative, warning), so / therefore (result), for (reason).

    📖 III. Compound Sentences
  1. A warning of what will happen if he is late: 'otherwise' (or 'or').

Answer: He must not be late, otherwise he will be punished.
