1QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

The passive of "People say that he is a thief" is:

  1. (a) He is said to be a thief. ✓
  2. (b) He said to be a thief.
  3. (c) He is saying a thief.
  4. (d) It said he is a thief.

Answer: (a) He is said to be a thief.

Show the explanation
  1. With verbs of reporting: It is said that … / subject + is said + to-infinitive. Both clauses are present, so "to be".

2QuestionSection A · 1 marksHard

The passive of "People say that he was an honest officer" is:

  1. (a) He is said to have been an honest officer. ✓
  2. (b) He is said to be an honest officer.
  3. (c) He was said to be an honest officer.
  4. (d) It says that he was honest.

Answer: (a) He is said to have been an honest officer.

Show the explanation
  1. The saying is present but the fact is past, so the infinitive becomes "to have been".

3QuestionSection A · 1 marksHard

The passive of "The teacher made him stand up" is:

  1. (a) He was made to stand up. ✓
  2. (b) He was made stand up.
  3. (c) He made to stand up.
  4. (d) He was making stand up.

Answer: (a) He was made to stand up.

Show the explanation
  1. The bare infinitive after make becomes a to-infinitive in the passive.

4QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

The passive of "Father gave the son fifty rupees" is:

  1. (a) The son was given fifty rupees. ✓
  2. (b) The son gave fifty rupees.
  3. (c) Fifty rupees gave the son.
  4. (d) The son was give fifty rupees.

Answer: (a) The son was given fifty rupees.

Show the explanation
  1. The nearest (indirect) object becomes the subject; the verb is was + V3 (given).

5QuestionSection A · 1 marksHard

Which sentence has no passive form?

  1. (a) The meeting lasted two hours. ✓
  2. (b) The police arrested the thief.
  3. (c) They built a bridge.
  4. (d) She opened the door.

Answer: (a) The meeting lasted two hours.

Show the explanation
  1. "last" is a link / intransitive verb: "two hours" is not an object, so there is no passive.

6QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

Why is there no agent in "The patient was taken to hospital"?

  1. (a) The agent is obvious or unimportant. ✓
  2. (b) The agent is a famous person.
  3. (c) The agent is unusual.
  4. (d) The agent is new information.

Answer: (a) The agent is obvious or unimportant.

Show the explanation
  1. We are interested in the patient, not in who carried him.

7QuestionSection B · 3 marksHard

Change into the passive voice:
(a) They have selected Rita captain of the team.
(b) We saw him crossing the road.
(c) You must obey the rules.

Answer: (a) Rita has been selected captain of the team. (b) He was seen crossing the road. (c) The rules must be obeyed.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) The object complement (captain) stays after the verb. (b) The -ing form stays. (c) modal + be + V3.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (B)

8QuestionSection B · 3 marksMedium

Change into the passive voice:
(a) Why did they postpone the meeting?
(b) When will you deliver the parcel?
(c) Where do they sell fresh fish?

Answer: (a) Why was the meeting postponed? (b) When will the parcel be delivered? (c) Where is fresh fish sold?

Show the explanation
  1. Keep the wh-word first; change the verb to the passive of the same tense (did → was + V3, will → will be + V3, do → is + V3).
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (B)

9QuestionSection B · 3 marksHard

Explain why these sentences cannot be changed into the passive:
(a) I have a car.
(b) The baby sleeps ten hours a day.
(c) My shoes fit me well.

Answer: (a) "have" meaning "possess" is a verb of state: it has no passive. (b) "sleep" is intransitive: "ten hours" is not an object. (c) "fit" is one of the verbs (fit, suit, resemble, lack) that are not passivised.

Show the explanation
  1. The chapter’s restrictions (a), (b) and (E). One mark for each reason.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: Restrictions

10QuestionSection B · 3 marksHard

Change into the passive and say whether you keep the agent:
(a) Valmiki wrote the Ramayan.
(b) Someone has eaten all the biscuits.
(c) A robot runs this hotel.

Answer: (a) The Ramayan was written by Valmiki — keep: an important author. (b) All the biscuits have been eaten — leave out: unknown. (c) This hotel is run by a robot — keep: an unusual agent.

Show the explanation
  1. The by-phrase is kept for important, new or unexpected agents and left out for unknown or obvious ones.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (5)

11QuestionSection C · 4 marksHard

Complete this news report with the passive forms of the verbs in brackets (½ mark each):
A bank in Cuttack _____ (rob) last night. Cash worth ten lakh rupees _____ (take) away. The security guard _____ (tie) up and _____ (lock) in a room. The robbery _____ (discover) in the morning when the bank _____ (open). The case _____ (investigate) by the police now. The robbers _____ (believe) to be from another state.

Answer: A bank in Cuttack was robbed last night. Cash worth ten lakh rupees was taken away. The security guard was tied up and locked in a room. The robbery was discovered in the morning when the bank was opened. The case is being investigated by the police now. The robbers are believed to be from another state.

Show the explanation
  1. Past events: was / were + V3 (the second "was" before "locked" may be left out).
  2. "now": is being + V3. A reporting verb: are believed + to-infinitive.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (5)

12QuestionSection C · 4 marksMedium

Describe how lemonade is made in four passive sentences.

Answer: Sample: First, a glass of water is taken. Two spoons of sugar are added and stirred well. Then the juice of half a lemon is squeezed into it. Finally, a pinch of salt is added and the lemonade is served cold.

Show the explanation
  1. A process is written in the present simple passive without the agent. One mark for each correct sentence.

13QuestionSection C · 4 marksHard

Correct the errors:
(a) The letter was wrote by him.
(b) A car is had by me.
(c) The work is being did.
(d) English is speak in many countries.

Answer: (a) The letter was written by him. (b) I have a car. (no passive is possible) (c) The work is being done. (d) English is spoken in many countries.

Show the explanation
  1. (a), (c), (d): the passive needs the past participle (V3), not V2 or V1.
  2. (b) "have" (= possess) cannot be made passive; keep the active sentence.
