1QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

English _____ in many countries.

  1. (a) is spoken ✓
  2. (b) speaks
  3. (c) is speaking
  4. (d) spoke

Answer: (a) English is spoken in many countries.

Show the explanation
  1. English does not speak; people speak it. A general fact in the passive: is + V3.

2QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

The thief _____ yesterday.

  1. (a) was caught ✓
  2. (b) caught
  3. (c) is caught
  4. (d) has caught

Answer: (a) The thief was caught yesterday.

Show the explanation
  1. "yesterday": past simple passive, was + V3.

3QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

Which sentence is in the passive voice?

  1. (a) She wrote a letter.
  2. (b) A letter was written by her. ✓
  3. (c) She is writing a letter.
  4. (d) She has written a letter.

Answer: (b) A letter was written by her.

Show the explanation
  1. Only this one has be + V3 (was written) with the object of the action as its subject.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (B)

  2. (b) will finish
  3. (c) is finishing
  4. (d) finished

Answer: (a) The work will be finished by tomorrow.

Show the explanation
  1. Future passive: will be + V3.

5QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

Our house _____ now.

  2. (b) is painting
  3. (c) was painted
  4. (d) has painted

Answer: (a) Our house is being painted now.

Show the explanation
  1. "now" — an action in progress, in the passive: is being + V3.

6QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

The passive form of "They play cricket" is:

  1. (a) Cricket is played by them. ✓
  2. (b) Cricket was played by them.
  3. (c) Cricket is being played by them.
  4. (d) Cricket has played by them.

Answer: (a) Cricket is played by them.

Show the explanation
  1. The active is present simple, so the passive is too: is + V3.

8QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

The letter _____ before I arrived.

  1. (a) had been posted ✓
  2. (b) has been posted
  3. (c) was posting
  4. (d) had posted

Answer: (a) The letter had been posted before I arrived.

Show the explanation
  1. The earlier of two past actions, in the passive: had been + V3.

9QuestionSection A · 1 marksEasy

In "My purse has been stolen", the agent is left out because it is:

  1. (a) unknown ✓
  2. (b) a famous person
  3. (c) unusual
  4. (d) new information

10QuestionSection A · 1 marksMedium

The active form of "Was the window broken by Ravi?" is:

  1. (a) Did Ravi break the window? ✓
  2. (b) Has Ravi broken the window?
  3. (c) Was Ravi breaking the window?
  4. (d) Ravi broke the window?

Answer: (a) Did Ravi break the window?

Show the explanation
  1. was + V3 corresponds to the past simple; a past-simple question uses did + V1.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (B)

11QuestionSection B · 2 marksEasy

Change into the passive voice:
(a) The cat killed the rat.
(b) She sings a song.

Answer: (a) The rat was killed by the cat. (b) A song is sung by her.

Show the explanation
  1. The object becomes the subject; the tense of the verb stays the same (killed → was killed, sings → is sung).
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (B)

12QuestionSection B · 2 marksEasy

Fill in the blanks with the passive forms of the verbs in brackets:
(a) Rice _____ (grow) in Odisha.
(b) The bridge _____ (build) in 2010.

Answer: (a) Rice is grown in Odisha. (b) The bridge was built in 2010.

Show the explanation
  1. (a) a general fact: is + V3. (b) a past event: was + V3.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (B)

13QuestionSection B · 2 marksMedium

Change into the passive voice:
(a) They are repairing the road.
(b) He has finished the work.

Answer: (a) The road is being repaired. (b) The work has been finished (by him).

Show the explanation
  1. (a) present progressive: is being + V3. (b) present perfect: has been + V3.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (B)

14QuestionSection B · 2 marksEasy

Write A for active and P for passive:

15QuestionSection B · 2 marksMedium

Change into the passive voice:
(a) Did she cook the food?
(b) Who broke the glass?

Answer: (a) Was the food cooked by her? (b) By whom was the glass broken?

Show the explanation
  1. Make each a statement, change it to the passive, then make the question again: Who … ? → By whom … ?

16QuestionSection C · 5 marksEasy

Rewrite in the passive voice:
(a) Columbus discovered America.
(b) The teacher praises good students.
(c) We will finish the project tomorrow.
(d) Somebody has stolen my bicycle.
(e) Farmers grow wheat in this village.

Answer: (a) America was discovered by Columbus. (b) Good students are praised by the teacher. (c) The project will be finished tomorrow. (d) My bicycle has been stolen. (e) Wheat is grown in this village.

Show the explanation
  1. Keep the tense of each active verb.
  2. Leave out "by us", "by somebody" and "by farmers": the agent is unimportant, unknown or obvious.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (B)

17QuestionSection C · 5 marksMedium

Complete this school notice with the passive forms of the verbs in brackets:
The school _____ (close) tomorrow on account of the festival. All students _____ (ask) to attend the prayer on Monday. Uniforms _____ (must / wear) every day. Mobile phones _____ (not / allow) in the classroom. Late comers _____ (will / punish).

Answer: The school will be closed tomorrow on account of the festival. All students are asked to attend the prayer on Monday. Uniforms must be worn every day. Mobile phones are not allowed in the classroom. Late comers will be punished.

Show the explanation
  1. Notices are written in the passive without the agent (the school authority is obvious).
  2. will / must + be + V3; present simple passive for rules.
← Back to the question 📖 Section: (5)
