1Question

Use one tea bag to make each cup of tea.

Rules used

  • Passive without the agent

    Use the passive without 'by …' when the agent is unknown, unimportant, obvious, or when we do not want to name him; it is common in official, scientific and newspaper writing.

    📖 (5) Use of the by + the agent phrase in the passive
  1. Done in the book as the example.
  2. Instructions describe a process, so they become present simple passive sentences without an agent.

Answer: One tea-bag is used for making each cup of tea.
