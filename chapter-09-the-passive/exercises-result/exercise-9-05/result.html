(ii)Question

People say he resigned his job, but he didn’t. (sack)

Rules used

  • Forming the passive

    The object of the active becomes the subject; the verb becomes be + V3 (past participle) in the same tense; 'by + agent' may be left out. is/am/are/was/were + V3 · is being / was being + V3 · has/have/had been + V3 · modal + be + V3 · modal + have been + V3.

    📖 The Passive (introduction) and (B)
  • Passive without the agent

    Use the passive without 'by …' when the agent is unknown, unimportant, obvious, or when we do not want to name him; it is common in official, scientific and newspaper writing.

    📖 (5) Use of the by + the agent phrase in the passive
  1. He did not resign; someone dismissed him.

Answer: He was sacked.
