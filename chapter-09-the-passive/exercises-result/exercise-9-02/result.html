(i)Question

A car will ( buy ) next year if the savings increase.

Rules used

  • Passive in different tenses

    Present simple: is made · Past simple: was made · Present progressive: is being made · Past progressive: was being made · Present perfect: has been made · Past perfect: had been made · Modals: will / should / must be made.

    📖 (B) Use of the Passive in different patterns of sentences
  1. Future: will be + V3.

Answer: A car will be bought next year if the savings increase.

(ii)Question

Thousands of people ( kill ) in the earthquake in Japan last year.

Rules used (as above)
  • Passive in different tenses

    Present simple: is made · Past simple: was made · Present progressive: is being made · Past progressive: was being made · Present perfect: has been made · Past perfect: had been made · Modals: will / should / must be made.

    📖 (B) Use of the Passive in different patterns of sentences
  1. 'last year': past simple passive, were + V3 (plural subject).

Answer: Thousands of people were killed in the earthquake in Japan last year.

(ix)Question

His grandfather ( say ) to have been a landlord.

Rules used

  • Passive in different tenses

    Present simple: is made · Past simple: was made · Present progressive: is being made · Past progressive: was being made · Present perfect: has been made · Past perfect: had been made · Modals: will / should / must be made.

    📖 (B) Use of the Passive in different patterns of sentences
  • Passive with verbs of reporting and infinitives

    It is said that … / He is said to have been … (a past fact → to have been). An infinitive can be passive too: to be left, to be painted.

    📖 (B) (xi) and (xiv)
  1. A verb of reporting: is said + to have been (a past fact).

Answer: His grandfather is said to have been a landlord.
