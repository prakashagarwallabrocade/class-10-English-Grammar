(i)Question

Originally, this novel (write) in Hindi, but it (translate) into odia in 1990.

Rules used

  • Passive in different tenses

    Present simple: is made · Past simple: was made · Present progressive: is being made · Past progressive: was being made · Present perfect: has been made · Past perfect: had been made · Modals: will / should / must be made.

    📖 (B) Use of the Passive in different patterns of sentences
  1. Two past events: was + V3.

Answer: Originally, this novel was written in Hindi, but it was translated into odia in 1990.
