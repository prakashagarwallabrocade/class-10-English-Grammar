(i)Question

Prakash killed a snake with a stick.

Rules used

  • Forming the passive

    The object of the active becomes the subject; the verb becomes be + V3 (past participle) in the same tense; 'by + agent' may be left out. is/am/are/was/were + V3 · is being / was being + V3 · has/have/had been + V3 · modal + be + V3 · modal + have been + V3.

    📖 The Passive (introduction) and (B)
  1. The subject 'Prakash' does the action (killed).

Answer: (A) active
